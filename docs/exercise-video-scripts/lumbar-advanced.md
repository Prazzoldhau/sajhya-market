# Low Back Pain (Lumbar) — Exercise Video Scripts

**Batch 3: Advanced (Level 3).** The 21 advanced-level Lumbar exercises
in the app (`Spine → Lumbar`, difficulty 3), mapped to their
`ExerciseMain` records by ID.

Other batches: [`lumbar-beginner.md`](lumbar-beginner.md) ·
[`lumbar-intermediate.md`](lumbar-intermediate.md).

---

## Two sections, on purpose

**A. Self-guided (full scripts).** Bodyweight and band work — planks,
supermans, dead bug / bird dog with resistance, Pallof press, single-leg
RDL. Same format and rules as the earlier batches.

**B. Supervised progression (technique reference only).** Loaded barbell,
kettlebell and machine lifts. These are **not** solo home exercises for a
back-pain patient — they're late-stage return-to-loading, done with a
physiotherapist or strength coach watching form in person. Any video here
is a *reference* for what good technique looks like, not a "watch this and
do it on your own" instruction. Each carries that on screen.

The physio still decides who gets what and when — nothing in Section B
should reach a patient who hasn't been cleared for loaded lifting.

Finished video → host → paste URL into that exercise's `hosted_video_url`
(admin → Exercise main → matching ID).

# Section A — Self-guided (full scripts)

## 1. Front Plank (Full) · app exercise #2294

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist in a full forearm plank, straight line head to heels | **"यो Full Plank हो। यसले पेट र ढाडका मांसपेशीलाई काम गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Front Plank (full) |
| 5–15s | On forearms and toes, elbows under the shoulders, feet hip-width | **"कुहिनोमा टेक्नुहोस्, कुहिनो काँधमुनि। पैतलाका औँलामा टेकेर उभिनुहोस्।"** | Forearms + toes · Elbows under shoulders |
| 15–25s | Lift into a straight line from head to heels, gentle abdominal + glute brace | **"पेट र चाक हल्का कस्दै शरीरलाई टाउकोदेखि कुर्कुच्चासम्म सिधा बनाउनुहोस्।"** | Straight line — head to heels |
| 25–35s | Hold, breathing, hips not sagging or piking up | **"यो स्थिति समात्नुहोस्, सास फेरिरहनुहोस्। नितम्ब नझर्नुहोस्, नउचाल्नुहोस्।"** | Hold · Keep breathing · Hips level |
| 35–43s | Lower the knees, then the hips, and rest | **"बिस्तारै घुँडा अनि नितम्ब तल झारेर आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Lower and rest |
| 43–50s | Repeat | **"यही चाल दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat |
| 50–55s | Safety screen | **"कम्मर झर्न थाल्यो वा दुख्न थाल्यो भने रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if the back sags or hurts |

**Don't include:** holding on while the low back sags — end the hold and rest.
**Modification:** drop to knees (that's the Level 2 version, #2288); or shorten the hold.
**Safety line:** कम्मर झर्न थाल्यो वा दुख्न थाल्यो भने रोक्नुहोस्।

## 2. Side Plank (Full) · app exercise #2295

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist in a full side plank, feet stacked, body straight | **"यो Full Side Plank हो। यसले कम्मरको छेउका मांसपेशीलाई काम गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Side Plank (full) |
| 5–15s | On one side, forearm under the shoulder, legs straight, feet stacked (or one in front of the other), top hand on the hip | **"एक छेउ ढल्किनुहोस्। कुहिनो काँधमुनि, खुट्टा सिधा, पैतला एकमाथि अर्को। माथिको हात कम्मरमा।"** | Elbow under shoulder · Feet stacked |
| 15–25s | Lift the hips into a straight line from head to feet | **"नितम्बलाई माथि उठाउनुहोस्, टाउकोदेखि पैतलासम्म सिधा लाइन।"** | Lift hips — straight line, head to feet |
| 25–33s | Hold, breathing, hips not dropping toward the floor | **"यो स्थिति समात्नुहोस्, सास फेरिरहनुहोस्। नितम्ब तल नझर्नुहोस्।"** | Hold · Hips don't drop |
| 33–40s | Lower with control | **"बिस्तारै नितम्ब तल झार्नुहोस्।"** | Lower slowly |
| 40–50s | Other side | **"अब अर्को छेउ गर्नुहोस्।"** | Now the other side |
| 50–55s | Safety screen | **"कम्मर वा काँधमा दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if back or shoulder pain increases |

**Don't include:** rolling forward or backward — the body stays in one flat plane, chest facing forward.
**Modification:** bottom knee down (Level 2 version, #2289); or put the top foot in front of the bottom one for a wider base.
**Safety line:** कम्मर वा काँधमा दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 3. Plank with Arm/Leg Lift · app exercise #2304

**Target: 50–60 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist in a full plank, lifting one limb at a time | **"यो Plank मा हात-खुट्टा उठाउने चाल हो। यसले plank लाई अझ चुनौतीपूर्ण बनाउँछ।"** | Plank with Arm/Leg Lift |
| 5–14s | Set up in a full forearm plank, straight line, feet a little wider than hip-width | **"Full Plank बनाउनुहोस्। पैतला अलि फराकिलो राख्नुहोस्।"** | Full plank · Feet slightly wide |
| 14–24s | Lift one arm forward a few cm — hips stay completely level, no rotation | **"बिस्तारै एउटा हात अगाडि हल्का उठाउनुहोस्। नितम्ब नबटारियोस्, तेर्सै।"** | Lift one arm · Hips stay level |
| 24–32s | Return the hand, then lift the opposite leg a few cm — hips still level | **"हात फर्काउनुहोस्, अनि विपरीत खुट्टा हल्का उठाउनुहोस्। नितम्ब उही तेर्सो।"** | Then the opposite leg |
| 32–45s | Alternate slowly, side + rear view | **"बिस्तारै पालैपालो गर्नुहोस्। कुनै झट्का नदिनुहोस्।"** | Alternate slowly |
| 45–53s | Lower to the floor, rest | **"बिस्तारै तल झारेर आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Lower and rest |
| 53–60s | Safety screen | **"नितम्ब बटारिन थाल्यो वा दुख्न थाल्यो भने रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if the hips twist or the back hurts |

**Don't include:** lifting the limb high or letting the hips rotate — the lift is small, the trunk stays still.
**Modification:** do it from a knees-down plank, or lift only the arm (not the leg) first.
**Safety line:** नितम्ब बटारिन थाल्यो वा दुख्न थाल्यो भने रोक्नुहोस्।

## 4. Dead Bug with Resistance · app exercise #2303

**Target: 50–60 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, holding a band (anchored behind the head) or a light weight, doing a dead bug | **"यो प्रतिरोध सहितको Dead Bug हो। ब्यान्ड वा हल्का तौलले पेटका मांसपेशीलाई बढी काम दिन्छ।"** | Dead Bug with resistance |
| 5–15s | Hook-lying, hips and knees at 90°, arms holding a band that's anchored above the head (or holding a light weight) | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, घुँडा ९० डिग्रीमा। टाउकोमाथि बाँधिएको ब्यान्ड दुवै हातले समात्नुहोस्।"** | Hips + knees at 90° · Hold the band overhead |
| 15–23s | Gentle abdominal brace, arms pulling lightly against the band, low back flat | **"नाभि हल्का भित्रतिर, ब्यान्डलाई हल्का तान्दै कम्मर भुइँमा टाँसिएको।"** | Brace · Low back stays flat |
| 23–35s | Extend one leg away (arms keep holding against the band) — back does not arch | **"एउटा खुट्टा बिस्तारै अगाडि फैलाउनुहोस्। हातले ब्यान्ड समातिरहनुहोस्, कम्मर स्थिर।"** | Extend one leg · Back stays flat |
| 35–44s | Return, other leg | **"फर्काउनुहोस्, अनि अर्को खुट्टा गर्नुहोस्।"** | Return · Then the other leg |
| 44–55s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest when done |
| 55–60s | Safety screen | **"कम्मर उठे वा दुखे रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if the back lifts or hurts |

**Don't include:** letting the band pull the arms overhead and the back into an arch — the arms hold firm, the back stays down.
**Modification:** drop the band — plain dead bug (#2272) — if the back can't stay flat.
**Safety line:** कम्मर उठे वा दुखे रोक्नुहोस्।

## 5. Supermans (Prone extension) · app exercise #2306

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist face down, lifting arms and legs a small way off the floor | **"यो Superman व्यायाम हो। यसले ढाडका पछाडिका मांसपेशीलाई काम गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Supermans |
| 5–13s | Face down, arms reaching forward, legs straight, forehead down | **"घोप्टो पल्टनुहोस्। हात अगाडि फैलाउनुहोस्, खुट्टा सिधा, निधार भुइँमा।"** | Lie face down · Arms forward |
| 13–24s | Lift both arms and both legs a small way off the floor at the same time — a small lift, gaze at the floor | **"दुवै हात र दुवै खुट्टा एकसाथ बिस्तारै थोरै माथि उठाउनुहोस्। हेराइ भुइँतिर।"** | Lift arms + legs a little · Look at the floor |
| 24–31s | Brief hold, breathing, neck long | **"छोटो समय समात्नुहोस्, घाँटी लामो। सास फेरिरहनुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Don't crane the neck |
| 31–40s | Lower with control | **"बिस्तारै तल झर्नुहोस्।"** | Lower slowly |
| 40–50s | Repeat | **"यही चाल दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat |
| 50–55s | Safety screen | **"कम्मरमा दुखाइ बढेमा वा खुट्टामा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if back pain increases or leg tingling |

**Don't include:** lifting the arms and legs high and hyper-arching the low back — the lift is small.
**Modification:** lift arms only, or legs only, not both — or do Alternating Supermans (#2307).
**Safety line:** कम्मरमा दुखाइ बढेमा वा खुट्टामा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 6. Alternating Supermans · app exercise #2307

**Target: 50–60 seconds.** The prone version of Bird Dog.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist face down, lifting one arm and the opposite leg | **"यो Alternating Superman हो। यसले ढाडलाई नियन्त्रणसहित काम गर्न सिकाउँछ।"** | Alternating Supermans |
| 5–13s | Face down, arms reaching forward, legs straight | **"घोप्टो पल्टनुहोस्। हात अगाडि, खुट्टा सिधा, निधार भुइँमा।"** | Lie face down · Arms forward |
| 13–25s | Lift one arm and the opposite leg a small way off the floor | **"बिस्तारै एउटा हात र त्यसको विपरीत खुट्टा थोरै माथि उठाउनुहोस्।"** | Opposite arm and leg — small lift |
| 25–33s | Brief hold, hips staying level on the floor, then lower | **"छोटो समय समात्नुहोस्, नितम्ब भुइँमै तेर्सो। अनि तल झर्नुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Hips stay level |
| 33–48s | Other side, alternate slowly | **"अब अर्को तर्फ। बिस्तारै पालैपालो गर्नुहोस्।"** | Alternate slowly |
| 48–55s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest when done |
| 55–60s | Safety screen | **"कम्मरमा दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if back pain increases |

**Don't include:** twisting the pelvis to lift the leg higher — the hips stay flat and level.
**Modification:** slide the limbs along the floor instead of lifting; add the lift later.
**Safety line:** कम्मरमा दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 8. Pallof Press (Kneeling or standing) · app exercise #2301

**Target: 50–60 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist side-on to a band anchor, pressing the band straight out from the chest, resisting the twist | **"यो Pallof Press हो। यसले शरीरलाई नबटारी स्थिर राख्ने मांसपेशीलाई काम दिन्छ।"** | Pallof Press |
| 5–16s | Standing (or half-kneeling) side-on to a band anchored at chest height, both hands holding the band at the chest, feet hip-width | **"ब्यान्डलाई छातीको उचाइमा बाँध्नुहोस्। छेउ फर्केर उभिनुहोस्, दुवै हातले ब्यान्ड छातीमा समात्नुहोस्।"** | Side-on to the anchor · Band at the chest |
| 16–27s | Press both hands straight forward — the band tries to rotate you; keep the hips and shoulders square | **"दुवै हात सिधा अगाडि धकेल्नुहोस्। ब्यान्डले बटार्न खोज्छ — नितम्ब र काँध अगाडितिरै राख्नुहोस्।"** | Press straight out · Don't let it twist you |
| 27–35s | Hold briefly at full reach, breathing, then bring the hands back to the chest | **"पूरा अगाडि पुगेर छोटो समय रोक्नुहोस्, अनि हात छातीमा फर्काउनुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Return to the chest |
| 35–48s | Repeat, then turn around and do the other side | **"दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्, अनि घुमेर अर्को तर्फ गर्नुहोस्।"** | Repeat · Then face the other way |
| 48–55s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest when done |
| 55–60s | Safety screen | **"शरीर बटारिन थाल्यो भने ब्यान्ड खुकुलो बनाउनुहोस्।"** | Twisting? Use a lighter band |

**Don't include:** letting the torso rotate toward the anchor as the arms extend — the whole point is to not rotate.
**Modification:** lighter band, stand closer to the anchor, or do it half-kneeling for a wider base.
**Safety line:** शरीर बटारिन थाल्यो भने ब्यान्ड खुकुलो बनाउनुहोस्।

## 9. Cable Anti-Rotation Hold · app exercise #2302

**Target: 45–55 seconds.** Same idea as the Pallof press, but a static hold.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist side-on to a cable/band, arms extended, holding still against the pull | **"यो Anti-Rotation Hold हो। यसले शरीरलाई नबटारी एकै ठाउँमा स्थिर राख्न सिकाउँछ।"** | Anti-Rotation Hold |
| 5–16s | Standing side-on to a cable (or band) at chest height, both hands holding it, arms pressed straight out from the chest | **"केबल वा ब्यान्डलाई छातीको उचाइमा। छेउ फर्केर उभिनुहोस्, दुवै हातले समातेर सिधा अगाडि तन्काउनुहोस्।"** | Side-on · Arms straight out from the chest |
| 16–30s | Hold that position — the cable pulls you toward it; keep the hips and shoulders square, don't rotate | **"त्यही स्थितिमा समात्नुहोस्। केबलले तान्छ — नितम्ब र काँध अगाडितिरै, नबटारियोस्।"** | Hold · Resist the pull · Stay square |
| 30–38s | Keep breathing throughout, then bring the hands in | **"सास फेरिरहनुहोस्, अनि हात भित्र ल्याउनुहोस्।"** | Keep breathing |
| 38–48s | Turn around, other side | **"घुमेर अर्को तर्फ गर्नुहोस्।"** | Then the other side |
| 48–55s | Safety screen | **"शरीर बटारिन थाल्यो भने प्रतिरोध घटाउनुहोस्।"** | Twisting? Reduce the resistance |

**Don't include:** holding the breath to brace — breathing continues through the hold.
**Modification:** a band instead of a cable; stand closer to the anchor for less pull.
**Safety line:** शरीर बटारिन थाल्यो भने प्रतिरोध घटाउनुहोस्।

**Target: 55–65 seconds.** Bodyweight balance version — no weight needed to learn it.

# Section B — Supervised progression (technique reference only)

**Do not treat these as home exercises.** They are loaded lifts for a
patient the physiotherapist has cleared for return-to-loading, coached in
person. A video here is a form reference, filmed with a coach in frame,
carrying a persistent on-screen line:

> **तपाईंको physiotherapist को उपस्थितिमा मात्र गर्नुहोस् — यो भिडियो हेरेर एक्लै नगर्नुहोस्।**
> *(Do only with your physiotherapist present — not alone from this video.)*

For each: what it is · the 3–4 form points a coach would cue · the hard
stop.

---

### 12. Deadlift (Light weight – Lumbar neutral) · #2296
**What:** Lifting a light weight from the floor by hinging at the hips with a flat back.
**Cue:** Bar/weight close to the shins · hips back, chest down, back flat (neutral, not rounded, not over-arched) · drive through the floor, hips and shoulders rise together · lower with the same flat back.
**Hard stop:** any back rounding under load, or pain — rack the weight, done.

### 13. Romanian Deadlift (Light weight) · #2297
**What:** A hip hinge with a light weight and only a slight knee bend, lowering to about mid-shin.
**Cue:** Slight knee bend, then fixed · push the hips back, weight slides down the thighs · back flat throughout · stop when the hamstrings tighten or the back wants to round · stand up hips-first.
**Hard stop:** back rounding, or a pulling/pinching in the low back.

### 14. Goblet Squat · #2298
**What:** A squat holding a single weight (kettlebell/dumbbell) at the chest.
**Cue:** Weight held tight to the chest, elbows down · hips back and down, chest up · knees track over the toes · depth only as far as the low back stays neutral (no tuck at the bottom) · stand up chest and hips together.
**Hard stop:** low back tucking/rounding at the bottom, or knee pain.

### 15. Back Squat (Barbell – Lumbar neutral) · #2311
**What:** A barbell squat with the bar across the upper back. **Gym setting, spotter or rack required.**
**Cue:** Bar settled on the upper back (not the neck) · brace before descending · hips and knees bend together, back neutral · depth stops before any pelvic tuck ("butt wink") · drive up evenly.
**Hard stop:** butt wink under load, any low-back pain, or losing the brace — re-rack.

### 16. Good Morning (Light barbell) · #2314
**What:** A hip hinge with a light barbell on the upper back. **Technically demanding — light load only, coached.**
**Cue:** Soft knees, fixed · hips push straight back, torso tips forward as one flat unit · small range to start · reverse by driving the hips forward.
**Hard stop:** back rounding, or the load drifting the torso down further than intended.

### 17. Kettlebell Swing (Lumbar neutral) · #2308
**What:** A ballistic hip hinge swinging a kettlebell to chest height. **Power move — form must be solid unloaded first.**
**Cue:** Hinge (not squat) · kettlebell "hikes" back between the thighs, forearms contact the hips · snap the hips forward to float the bell up · back stays flat the whole time · let the bell fall back into the next hinge.
**Hard stop:** using the low back or arms to lift the bell instead of the hips, or any back pain.

### 18. Farmer's Walk (Heavy) · #2309
**What:** Walking while carrying a heavy weight in each hand.
**Cue:** Stand tall, ribs down, shoulders back · brace the trunk · short, controlled steps · don't lean forward or arch back · set the weights down with a flat-back hinge, not a round-back dump.
**Hard stop:** trunk collapsing forward or sideways, grip failing near people/feet.

### 19. Suitcase Carry (Unilateral load) · #2310
**What:** A farmer's walk with weight in **one** hand only — resisting a side bend.
**Cue:** Weight in one hand · stand perfectly upright, do not let the loaded side pull you into a lean · shoulders and hips level · walk, then switch hands · set down with a flat back.
**Hard stop:** visible side-lean you can't correct, or low-back pain on the loaded side.

### 20. Hyperextension (Back extension bench) · #2312
**What:** On a 45° or horizontal back-extension bench, lowering and raising the torso. **Gym equipment.**
**Cue:** Hips on the pad, ankles secured · lower with a flat back by hinging at the hips · raise back to a straight line — **not** past it into an arch · slow and controlled, no bouncing.
**Hard stop:** arching past neutral at the top, or any pinch in the low back.

### 21. Reverse Hyperextension (Roman chair) · #2313
**What:** Torso supported on a pad/bench, lifting the legs backward from hanging. **Gym equipment.**
**Cue:** Torso and hips supported, legs hang down · lift the legs backward to about a straight line with the torso · **stop at horizontal**, don't swing higher · lower with control.
**Hard stop:** swinging the legs up past horizontal (hyper-arching the low back), or momentum taking over.
