# Knee — Exercise Video Scripts

**Batch 3: Advanced (Level 3).** The 5 advanced-level Knee exercises in
the app (`Lower Limb → Knee`, difficulty 3), mapped to their
`ExerciseMain` records by ID.

Other batches: [`knee-beginner.md`](knee-beginner.md) ·
[`knee-intermediate.md`](knee-intermediate.md).

**These are late-stage return-to-loading exercises.** All bodyweight (no
barbell), so they get full scripts — but they demand real single-leg
strength, balance and mobility, and one (Nordic curl) has a high eccentric
load that causes heavy soreness if progressed too fast. Every script
carries:

> **यो व्यायाम तपाईंको physiotherapist ले उन्नत चरणका लागि छनोट गरेपछि मात्र गर्नुहोस्।**
> *(Do this only once your physiotherapist has cleared you for advanced-stage work.)*

Plus the standard knee safety line:

> **घुँडा धेरै सुन्निएमा, तीखो दुखाइ भएमा, वा घुँडा लर्खराउन थालेमा रोक्नुहोस्।**

## 1. Bulgarian Split Squat · app exercise #2359

**Target: 60–70 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist in a split stance, rear foot up on a bench, lowering the back knee toward the floor, front knee to ~90° | **"यो Bulgarian Split Squat हो — पछाडिको खुट्टा बेन्चमा राखेर एक खुट्टाको Squat।"** | Bulgarian Split Squat |
| 6–18s | Standing about two feet in front of a knee-height bench, place the top of the rear foot on it, hands forward or on the hips | **"घुँडाकै उचाइको बेन्चको करिब दुई फुट अगाडि उभिनुहोस्। पछाडिको पैतलाको माथिल्लो भाग बेन्चमा राख्नुहोस्।"** | Rear foot on a knee-height bench · Two feet in front |
| 18–20s | Check balance first, hold a rail if needed | **"पहिले सन्तुलन जाँच्नुहोस्, चाहिए रेलिङ समात्नुहोस्।"** | Check balance first · Hold a rail if needed |
| 20–34s | Lower straight down by bending the front knee — front shin fairly vertical, front knee tracking over the mid-foot, torso upright with a slight forward lean | **"अगाडिको घुँडा खुम्च्याएर सिधा तल जानुहोस् — अगाडिको नली प्रायः ठाडो, घुँडा पैतलामाथि, शरीर हल्का अगाडि झुकेको।"** | Lower straight down · Front shin vertical · Knee over the foot |
| 34–44s | Drive up through the front heel to standing | **"अगाडिको कुर्कुच्चाको बलले माथि उभिनुहोस्।"** | Drive up through the front heel |
| 44–58s | Repeat, then the other leg | **"दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्, अनि अर्को खुट्टा।"** | Repeat · Then the other leg |
| 58–65s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest when done |
| 65–70s | Safety screen | **"अगाडिको घुँडा भित्र खुम्चियो, दुख्यो, वा लर्खरायो भने bench तल राख्नुहोस् वा Reverse Lunge मा फर्कनुहोस्।"** | Front knee caving, painful, or giving way? Lower bench, or back to reverse lunges |

**Don't include:** the front knee caving inward, the torso collapsing forward, or a very deep drop early on — build depth gradually.
**Modification:** a lower rear support (a step, not a bench); hold a rail; partial depth first.
**Safety line:** घुँडामा तीखो दुखाइ वा लर्खराएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 2. Cossack Squat · app exercise #2368

**Target: 55–65 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist in a wide stance, shifting side to side, squatting down over one bent leg while the other stays straight | **"यो Cossack Squat हो — फराकिलो उभिएर एकातिर तल बस्ने, अर्को खुट्टा सिधा।"** | Cossack Squat |
| 6–16s | Standing with the feet much wider than the shoulders, toes slightly out, hands forward for balance | **"खुट्टालाई काँधभन्दा धेरै फराकिलो राख्नुहोस्, औँला हल्का बाहिर, हात अगाडि।"** | Wide stance · Toes slightly out |
| 16–30s | Shift the hips toward one side and bend that knee, sinking down over that leg — the other leg stays straight, its toes may lift so the heel stays down | **"नितम्बलाई एकातिर सारेर त्यही घुँडा खुम्च्याउँदै तल बस्नुहोस्। अर्को खुट्टा सिधा — कुर्कुच्चा भुइँमा राख्न औँला उठ्न सक्छ।"** | Sink over one bent leg · Other leg straight · Heel down |
| 30–40s | Only as deep as the back stays fairly upright and the bending knee tracks over the foot | **"जति तल जाँदा ढाड प्रायः सिधा रहन्छ र घुँडा पैतलामाथि रहन्छ त्यति मात्र।"** | Depth: while the back stays upright and the knee tracks |
| 40–52s | Push back up to the wide stance, then shift to the other side | **"फेरि फराकिलो स्थितिमा माथि आउनुहोस्, अनि अर्को तर्फ जानुहोस्।"** | Push back up · Then the other side |
| 52–60s | Repeat slowly | **"बिस्तारै दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat slowly |
| 60–65s | Safety screen | **"घुँडा वा कम्मरमा तीखो दुखाइ भएमा कम तल जानुहोस्।"** | Don't go as low if sharp knee or back pain |

**Don't include:** bouncing at the bottom, or the back rounding hard to reach lower — controlled, upright.
**Modification:** hold a rail or a doorframe and lower only partway; a smaller stance width.
**Safety line:** घुँडा वा कम्मरमा तीखो दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 3. Poliquin Step-up · app exercise #2363

**Target: 55–65 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist facing away from a box, one foot on the box behind, stepping up using only that leg (the floor leg hangs, toes just brushing) | **"यो Poliquin Step-up हो — बाकसतिर पछाडि फर्केर, एउटै खुट्टाको बलले माथि जाने।"** | Poliquin Step-up |
| 6–18s | Standing with the back to a low box, place one foot flat on the box behind, the other leg's toes lightly on the floor in front, hands forward | **"तल्लो बाकसतिर पछाडि फर्केर उभिनुहोस्। एउटा पैतला पछाडि बाकसमा, अर्को खुट्टाको औँला अगाडि भुइँमा हल्का।"** | Back to a low box · One foot on the box behind |
| 18–20s | Hold a rail for balance to start | **"सुरुमा सन्तुलनका लागि रेलिङ समात्नुहोस्।"** | Hold a rail to start |
| 20–34s | Straighten the box-leg's knee to lift the body up — the front leg does NOT push off the floor, its toes stay light | **"बाकसमा भएको घुँडा सिधा गर्दै शरीरलाई माथि उठाउनुहोस् — अगाडिको खुट्टाले भुइँबाट धक्का नदेओस्।"** | Lift with the box leg only · Front foot stays light |
| 34–44s | Lower slowly and with control back to the start — the box leg lowers you | **"बिस्तारै र नियन्त्रणसहित तल फर्कनुहोस् — बाकसको खुट्टाले तल झार्छ।"** | Lower slowly · Control it with the box leg |
| 44–56s | Repeat, then the other leg | **"दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्, अनि अर्को खुट्टा।"** | Repeat · Then the other leg |
| 56–65s | Safety screen | **"घुँडा भित्र खुम्चियो, दुख्यो वा लर्खरायो भने तल्लो बाकस प्रयोग गर्नुहोस्।"** | Knee caving, painful, or giving way? Use a lower box |

**Don't include:** the front foot pushing off the floor to cheat the lift, or the box-leg knee caving inward.
**Modification:** a lower box; keep light rail support throughout; start with just the lowering half.
**Safety line:** घुँडामा तीखो दुखाइ वा लर्खराएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 4. Nordic Hamstring Curl · app exercise #2362

**Target: 60–70 seconds.** High eccentric load — progress slowly, expect soreness.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist kneeling with the ankles held down (partner or anchor), lowering the torso slowly toward the floor, catching with the hands, pushing back up | **"यो Nordic Curl हो — घुँडा टेकेर, गोलीगाँठो अड्काएर शरीरलाई बिस्तारै अगाडि झार्ने। तिघ्राको पछाडिलाई कडा काम दिन्छ।"** | Nordic Hamstring Curl (heavy on the hamstrings) |
| 6–18s | Kneeling upright on a padded surface, the ankles firmly held down by a partner (or under a solid anchor), body in a straight line from knees to head, hands ready in front of the chest | **"नरम सतहमा सिधा घुँडा टेक्नुहोस्, गोलीगाँठोलाई सहयोगीले वा बलियो ठाउँमुनि अड्काउनुहोस्। घुँडादेखि टाउकोसम्म सिधा, हात छातीअगाडि तयार।"** | Kneel tall · Ankles firmly held · Hands ready |
| 18–34s | Lower the whole body forward as ONE straight line, as slowly as possible, resisting the fall with the hamstrings | **"पूरै शरीरलाई एउटै सिधा लाइनमा, सकेसम्म बिस्तारै अगाडि झार्नुहोस्, तिघ्राको पछाडिले रोक्दै।"** | Lower as one straight line · As slow as possible |
| 34–40s | When the hamstrings can't hold any more, catch the fall softly with the hands | **"तिघ्राको पछाडिले नसक्ने भएपछि, हातले नरम रूपमा समात्नुहोस्।"** | Catch softly with the hands |
| 40–52s | Push back up with the hands to take the load off, then use the hamstrings to return to kneeling tall | **"हातले धकेलेर भार हटाउनुहोस्, अनि तिघ्राको पछाडिको बलले सिधा घुँडा टेक्ने स्थितिमा फर्कनुहोस्।"** | Push up with the hands · Then hamstrings back to tall |
| 52–62s | Repeat only a few controlled reps | **"थोरै, नियन्त्रित पटक मात्र दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Only a few controlled reps |
| 62–70s | Safety screen | **"तिघ्राको पछाडि तीखो, तान्ने खालको दुखाइ भएमा तुरुन्तै रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop at once if sharp, pulling pain in the back of the thigh |

**Don't include:** letting the body drop fast, or bending only at the hips (piking) — one straight line, controlled all the way.
**Modification:** start with a very small range near the top only; use a band or the hands on a bench to assist the lower; the physio sets the progression.
**Safety line:** तिघ्राको पछाडि तीखो दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 5. Shrimp Squat · app exercise #2370

**Target: 60–70 seconds.** Advanced single-leg strength + balance + mobility.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist on one leg, holding the other ankle behind, squatting down until the back knee lightly touches the floor, then standing back up | **"यो Shrimp Squat हो — एउटा खुट्टामा, अर्को कुर्कुच्चा पछाडि समातेर, पछाडिको घुँडाले भुइँ छुन्जेल तल बस्ने।"** | Shrimp Squat |
| 6–18s | Standing on one leg, reach back and hold that side's ankle, drawing the heel toward the buttock; the other arm is forward for balance | **"एउटा खुट्टामा उभिनुहोस्, त्यही तर्फको कुर्कुच्चा पछाडिबाट समात्नुहोस्। अर्को हात सन्तुलनका लागि अगाडि।"** | Stand on one leg · Hold the ankle behind |
| 18–20s | Balance first; a rail or wall nearby | **"पहिले सन्तुलन; नजिकै रेलिङ वा भित्ता राख्नुहोस्।"** | Balance first · Rail or wall nearby |
| 20–36s | Bend the standing knee and sit the hips back, lowering slowly until the raised knee lightly taps the floor behind — torso leans forward to counterbalance | **"उभिएको घुँडा खुम्च्याउँदै नितम्ब पछाडि बसाल्नुहोस्, बिस्तारै तल जानुहोस्, जबसम्म पछाडिको घुँडाले भुइँ हल्का छुँदैन। सन्तुलनका लागि शरीर अगाडि झुक्छ।"** | Lower slowly · Back knee taps the floor · Lean forward to balance |
| 36–48s | Drive up through the standing heel to return to one-leg standing | **"उभिएको कुर्कुच्चाको बलले माथि उठेर एक खुट्टामा फर्कनुहोस्।"** | Drive up through the standing heel |
| 48–60s | Repeat, then the other leg | **"दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्, अनि अर्को खुट्टा।"** | Repeat · Then the other leg |
| 60–65s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest when done |
| 65–70s | Safety screen | **"घुँडा भित्र खुम्चियो, दुख्यो, वा सन्तुलन बिग्रियो भने रेलिङ समातेर आधा दूरी मात्र गर्नुहोस्।"** | Knee caving, painful, or losing balance? Hold a rail, half depth only |

**Don't include:** crashing the back knee onto the floor, or the standing knee caving inward — a light tap, knee tracking over the foot.
**Modification:** hold a rail with one hand; lower onto a stack of cushions so there's less range; don't hold the rear ankle — let that foot trail.
**Safety line:** घुँडामा तीखो दुखाइ वा सन्तुलन बिग्रिएमा रोक्नुहोस्।
