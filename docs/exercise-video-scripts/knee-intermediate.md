## 1. Single Leg Glute Bridge · app exercise #2369

**Target: 50–60 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist in a bridge on one leg, hips level, other leg straight | **"यो एक खुट्टाको Bridge हो — सामान्य Bridge को गाह्रो रूप।"** | Single Leg Glute Bridge |
| 5–13s | Hook-lying, then extend one leg straight out (in line with the thigh of the bent leg) | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्। एउटा खुट्टालाई सिधा फैलाउनुहोस्।"** | One knee bent · Other leg straight |
| 13–24s | Push through the heel of the bent leg to lift the hips to a straight line — hips stay perfectly level | **"खुम्च्याइएको खुट्टाको कुर्कुच्चाको बलले नितम्ब उठाउनुहोस्, सिधा लाइनसम्म। नितम्ब एकातिर नझुकोस्।"** | Lift through the heel · Hips stay level |
| 24–32s | Brief hold, breathing | **"छोटो समय समात्नुहोस्, सास फेरिरहनुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Keep breathing |
| 32–42s | Lower slowly, repeat, then the other leg | **"बिस्तारै तल झार्नुहोस्, दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्, अनि अर्को खुट्टा।"** | Lower slowly · Repeat · Then the other leg |
| 42–52s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest when done |
| 52–60s | Safety screen | **"नितम्ब एकातिर झुक्यो भने दुई खुट्टाकै Bridge मा फर्कनुहोस्।"** | Hips dropping? Back to the two-leg bridge |

**Don't include:** the pelvis tipping toward the free-leg side — if it can't stay level, this is too soon.
**Modification:** keep the free-leg knee bent with the foot near the other knee, rather than a straight leg.
**Safety line:** घुँडा वा कम्मरमा दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 2. Contract-Relax (PNF) Knee Flexion Stretch · app exercise #3050

**Target: 55–65 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist with the knee bent to a stretch, briefly pushing the shin into a strap/hand, then relaxing and bending further | **"यो घुँडा खुम्च्याउने stretch मा बीचमा तिघ्राको मांसपेशी कस्ने, अनि छोडेर अझ खुम्च्याउने विधि हो।"** | Contract-Relax — knee flexion |
| 6–16s | Long-sitting, a strap around the foot (or a partner), the knee bent toward a comfortable end range of flexion | **"खुट्टा फैलाएर बस्नुहोस्, पैतलामा पेटी। घुँडालाई आरामदायी अन्तिम बिन्दुसम्म खुम्च्याउनुहोस्।"** | Knee bent toward its end range |
| 16–26s | Gently push the foot forward into the strap (trying to straighten the knee) at about 20–30% effort for 5–10 seconds — nothing moves | **"पैतलालाई पेटीविरुद्ध अगाडि हल्का धकेल्नुहोस् (घुँडा सिधा गर्ने प्रयास), ५–१० सेकेन्ड — केही नचलोस्।"** | Push into the strap 5–10s · Nothing moves |
| 26–34s | Relax fully for a breath, then gently draw the knee into a little more bend | **"एक सास पूरै छोड्नुहोस्, अनि घुँडालाई बिस्तारै अलि बढी खुम्च्याउनुहोस्।"** | Fully relax · Then bend a little further |
| 34–52s | Repeat the contract–relax cycle 2–3 times, each time gaining a little range | **"यही कस्ने–छोड्ने चक्र २–३ पटक दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्, हरेकपटक थोरै बढी।"** | Repeat the cycle · A little more each time |
| 52–60s | Ease out slowly | **"बिस्तारै बाहिर निस्कनुहोस्।"** | Ease out slowly |
| 60–65s | Safety screen | **"तीखो दुखाइ वा घुँडा सुन्निएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if sharp pain or swelling |

**Don't include:** a maximal push during the contract phase, or forcing the new range hard — gentle contraction, gentle gain.
**Modification:** the plain assisted stretch (#3038 / #3047) with no contract phase if this is confusing.
**Safety line:** तीखो दुखाइ वा सुन्निने भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 3. Contract-Relax (PNF) Knee Extension Stretch · app exercise #3051

**Target: 55–65 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist with the knee near full straight, briefly contracting the hamstrings, then relaxing and straightening further | **"यो घुँडा सिधा गर्ने stretch मा बीचमा तिघ्राको पछाडिको मांसपेशी कस्ने, अनि छोडेर अझ सिधा गर्ने विधि हो।"** | Contract-Relax — knee extension |
| 6–16s | Long-sitting, heel propped on a rolled towel so the knee is unsupported and near straight, or a strap under the foot | **"खुट्टा फैलाएर बस्नुहोस्, कुर्कुच्चा बेरेको तौलियामा, घुँडामुनि केही नराखी प्रायः सिधा।"** | Heel propped · Knee near straight, unsupported |
| 16–26s | Gently press the back of the knee / heel downward, trying to "dig the heel in", at 20–30% effort for 5–10 seconds | **"घुँडाको पछाडि / कुर्कुच्चालाई तल हल्का थिच्नुहोस्, ५–१० सेकेन्ड।"** | Gently press down 5–10s |
| 26–34s | Relax fully for a breath, then let (or gently assist) the knee settle a little straighter | **"एक सास पूरै छोड्नुहोस्, अनि घुँडालाई बिस्तारै अलि बढी सिधा हुन दिनुहोस्।"** | Fully relax · Then let it straighten a little more |
| 34–52s | Repeat the cycle 2–3 times | **"यही चक्र २–३ पटक दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat the cycle |
| 52–60s | Ease out | **"बिस्तारै बाहिर निस्कनुहोस्।"** | Ease out slowly |
| 60–65s | Safety screen | **"घुँडा पछाडि तीखो दुखाइ वा खुट्टामा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if sharp back-of-knee pain or leg tingling |

**Don't include:** bouncing, or a hard downward press on the thigh from outside — controlled, gentle.
**Modification:** the passive gravity versions (#3055 / #3040) with no contract phase.
**Safety line:** तीखो दुखाइ वा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 4. Post-Surgical Scar Mobilization (Knee) · app exercise #3052

**Target: 45–55 seconds.** Once the wound is fully healed and the physio has cleared it.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist gently massaging a healed knee scar in small circles with the fingertips | **"यो घाउ पूरै निको भएपछि, दाग नरम राख्न औँलाले हल्का मालिस गर्ने अभ्यास हो।"** | Scar Mobilization (healed scars only) |
| 6–16s | Long-sitting, leg relaxed, a healed scar visible; a little moisturiser or oil on the fingertips | **"खुट्टा खुकुलो राखेर बस्नुहोस्। औँलामा थोरै मोइस्चराइजर वा तेल लगाउनुहोस्।"** | Leg relaxed · A little oil on the fingertips |
| 16–30s | With one or two fingertips, move the scar and the skin around it in small circles — then gently glide it up/down and side to side | **"एक-दुई औँलाले दाग र वरिपरिको छालालाई सानो गोलो घुमाउनुहोस् — अनि हल्का माथि-तल, दायाँ-बायाँ सार्नुहोस्।"** | Small circles · Then glide up/down, side to side |
| 30–42s | The pressure is light-to-moderate, enough to move the tissue, not enough to hurt or redden a lot | **"दबाब हल्का-मध्यम — तन्तु चल्ने जति, तर दुख्ने वा धेरै रातो हुने जति होइन।"** | Light–moderate pressure — should not hurt |
| 42–50s | Wipe the oil off, rest | **"तेल पुछेर आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Wipe off · Rest |
| 50–55s | Safety screen | **"घाउ पूरै निको नभएसम्म, वा दाग तातो-रातो भए नगर्नुहोस् — physiotherapist लाई भन्नुहोस्।"** | Don't do this until fully healed; stop and tell your physio if the scar is hot or red |

**Don't include:** working an open, scabbed, or weeping wound — healed skin only.
**Modification:** lighter pressure; less time; the physio will show which directions matter for your scar.
**Safety line:** घाउ निको नभए वा दाग तातो-रातो भए नगर्नुहोस्।

## 5. Static Progressive Knee Extension Stretch (Splint/Brace) · app exercise #3053

**Target: 40–50 seconds.** Uses a prescribed knee extension splint/brace.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist wearing a knee extension brace, adjusting the dial to a comfortable end-range stretch and settling in | **"यो physiotherapist ले दिएको घुँडा सिधा गर्ने brace लगाएर गरिने stretch हो।"** | Static Progressive Extension (prescribed brace) |
| 6–18s | Sitting or lying with the leg supported, the extension brace fitted as the physio showed | **"खुट्टालाई सहारामा राखेर बस्नुहोस् वा पल्टनुहोस्। physiotherapist ले देखाएअनुसार brace लगाउनुहोस्।"** | Leg supported · Brace fitted as shown |
| 18–30s | Set the brace to a comfortable stretch into straightening — a firm pull, not a painful one | **"brace लाई आरामदायी तनावमा मिलाउनुहोस् — कडा तान, तर दुख्ने होइन।"** | Set to a firm — not painful — stretch |
| 30–40s | Stay for the prescribed time; if it eases off during the session, add a small amount of tension | **"तोकिएको समयसम्म रहनुहोस्; बीचमा तनाव घटे थोरै बढाउनुहोस्।"** | Hold for the prescribed time · Add a little tension if it eases |
| 40–46s | Release the brace slowly and take it off | **"brace लाई बिस्तारै खुकुलो बनाएर निकाल्नुहोस्।"** | Release slowly · Take it off |
| 46–50s | Safety screen | **"खुट्टामा झमझमाहट, चिसो, वा रंग बदलिएमा तुरुन्तै निकाल्नुहोस्।"** | Take it off at once if the leg tingles, goes cold, or changes colour |

**Don't include:** cranking the brace to a painful tension — progress is slow, over sessions.
**Modification:** the physio sets the starting tension, the hold time, and how fast to progress.
**Safety line:** खुट्टामा झमझमाहट, चिसो वा रंग बदलिएमा निकाल्नुहोस्।

## 6. Wall Sit · app exercise #2354

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist in a wall sit at about 90°, back flat on the wall | **"यो Wall Sit हो। यसले तिघ्रा र घुँडाका मांसपेशीलाई काम गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Wall Sit |
| 5–14s | Standing with the back against a wall, feet about a foot forward, hip-width | **"भित्तामा ढाड टेकाएर उभिनुहोस्, पैतला अगाडि करिब एक फुट।"** | Back on the wall · Feet a step forward |
| 14–24s | Slide down until the hips and knees are near 90° (or higher if the knee complains), knees over the ankles | **"भित्तामा घिस्रिँदै तल झर्नुहोस्, जबसम्म घुँडा करिब ९० डिग्री नहुन्छ — घुँडा दुखे अलि माथि। घुँडा गोलीगाँठोमाथि।"** | Slide to ~90° (higher if the knee complains) |
| 24–34s | Hold still, breathing, weight through the heels | **"स्थिर समात्नुहोस्, सास फेरिरहनुहोस्, तौल कुर्कुच्चामा।"** | Hold still · Weight through the heels |
| 34–42s | Slide back up, rest | **"भित्तामा घिस्रिँदै माथि उभिनुहोस्, आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Slide up · Rest |
| 42–50s | Repeat | **"यही चाल दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat |
| 50–55s | Safety screen | **"घुँडा अगाडि तीखो दुखाइ भएमा कम तल जानुहोस्।"** | Don't go as low if sharp front-knee pain |

**Don't include:** the knees going well past the toes, or sliding so low the knees hurt — a shallower sit is fine.
**Modification:** a higher hold (shallower bend); a gym ball behind the back for smoother sliding.
**Safety line:** घुँडामा तीखो दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 7. Terminal Knee Extension (TKE) · app exercise #2353

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist standing, band behind the knee anchored to a post in front, straightening the knee against the band | **"यो Terminal Knee Extension हो — घुँडाको अन्तिम सिधाइलाई प्रतिरोधविरुद्ध अभ्यास गर्ने।"** | Terminal Knee Extension (TKE) |
| 5–16s | Standing, a resistance band around the back of the knee, anchored to a post in front at knee height, stepped back so the band pulls the knee into a slight bend | **"उभिनुहोस्, ब्यान्डलाई घुँडाको पछाडि बेरेर अगाडि खम्बामा घुँडाकै उचाइमा अड्काउनुहोस्। ब्यान्डले घुँडा हल्का खुम्च्याउने गरी पछाडि सर्नुहोस्।"** | Band behind the knee · Anchored in front |
| 16–28s | Tighten the thigh and straighten the knee fully against the band — the heel stays on the floor, the other leg does little | **"तिघ्रा कस्दै ब्यान्डविरुद्ध घुँडा पूरै सिधा गर्नुहोस्। कुर्कुच्चा भुइँमै।"** | Straighten fully against the band · Heel stays down |
| 28–38s | Brief hold straight, then let the knee bend a little with control | **"सिधा भएर छोटो समय समात्नुहोस्, अनि नियन्त्रणसहित घुँडा हल्का खुम्च्याउनुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Then let it bend a little |
| 38–48s | Repeat, then the other leg if prescribed | **"दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्, अनि तोकिएको भए अर्को खुट्टा।"** | Repeat |
| 48–55s | Safety screen | **"घुँडा अगाडि तीखो दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if sharp front-knee pain |

**Don't include:** snapping the knee back into hyperextension, or lifting the heel — firm straighten, heel down.
**Modification:** lighter band; do the seated quad set (#3044) for the same feeling first.
**Safety line:** घुँडामा तीखो दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 8. Heel Elevated Squat · app exercise #2367

**Target: 50–60 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist squatting with the heels raised on a small wedge / plate, more upright torso, knees travelling forward | **"यो कुर्कुच्चा उठाएर गरिने Squat हो — तिघ्राको अगाडिलाई बढी काम दिन्छ।"** | Heel Elevated Squat |
| 5–15s | Standing, heels on a small plate or a rolled edge of a mat, feet shoulder-width | **"खुट्टा काँध जत्तिको चौडा, कुर्कुच्चा सानो प्लेट वा म्याटको बेरेको किनारामा।"** | Heels on a small raise · Feet shoulder-width |
| 15–27s | Squat down, letting the knees travel forward over the toes and the torso stay fairly upright — weight stays a little more forward | **"तल बस्नुहोस्, घुँडालाई औँलामाथि अगाडि जान दिनुहोस्, शरीर प्रायः सिधा। तौल हल्का अगाडि।"** | Knees travel forward · Torso stays upright |
| 27–35s | Only as deep as it stays comfortable and controlled | **"जति तल जाँदा आरामदायी र नियन्त्रित हुन्छ त्यति मात्र।"** | Comfortable, controlled depth only |
| 35–45s | Stand back up through the feet | **"पैतलाको बलले सिधा उभिनुहोस्।"** | Stand up through the feet |
| 45–54s | Repeat with control | **"यही चाल नियन्त्रणसहित दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat with control |
| 54–60s | Safety screen | **"घुँडा अगाडि तीखो दुखाइ भएमा कम तल जानुहोस् वा सामान्य Squat गर्नुहोस्।"** | Sharp front-knee pain? Less depth, or a normal squat |

**Don't include:** a very high heel wedge or forcing depth — a small raise, moderate depth.
**Modification:** a smaller heel raise; hold a rail for balance; do a normal bodyweight squat (#2349) if the front of the knee is irritable.
**Safety line:** घुँडामा तीखो दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 9. Reverse Lunge · app exercise #2360

**Target: 50–60 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist stepping one leg back into a lunge, lowering the back knee toward the floor, then pushing back to standing | **"यो Reverse Lunge हो। यसले घुँडा, तिघ्रा र नितम्बलाई काम दिन सक्छ।"** | Reverse Lunge |
| 5–14s | Standing tall, feet hip-width, hands on the hips (or holding a rail) | **"सिधा उभिनुहोस्, खुट्टा कम्मर जत्तिको चौडा, हात कम्मरमा वा रेलिङमा।"** | Stand tall · Feet hip-width |
| 14–26s | Step one foot straight back and lower the back knee toward the floor — front shin stays fairly vertical, front knee over the mid-foot | **"एउटा पैतला सिधा पछाडि सारेर पछाडिको घुँडालाई भुइँतिर झार्नुहोस्। अगाडिको नली प्रायः ठाडो, घुँडा पैतलामाथि।"** | Step back · Lower the back knee · Front shin vertical |
| 26–36s | Push through the front heel to step the back foot forward to standing | **"अगाडिको कुर्कुच्चाको बलले पछाडिको पैतला अगाडि ल्याएर उभिनुहोस्।"** | Push through the front heel · Return to standing |
| 36–50s | Alternate legs, slow and controlled | **"पालैपालो दुवै खुट्टामा, बिस्तारै र नियन्त्रणसहित।"** | Alternate legs · Slow and controlled |
| 50–56s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest when done |
| 56–60s | Safety screen | **"अगाडिको घुँडा भित्र खुम्चियो, दुख्यो, वा लर्खरायो भने दूरी घटाउनुहोस्।"** | Front knee caving, painful, or giving way? Shorten the step |

## 11. Walking Lunge · app exercise #2350

**Target: 50–60 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist stepping forward into a lunge, lowering the back knee, then stepping the back foot through into the next lunge | **"यो Walking Lunge हो — अगाडि बढ्दै पालैपालो lunge गर्ने।"** | Walking Lunge |
| 5–14s | Standing tall in a clear space, hands on the hips | **"खुला ठाउँमा सिधा उभिनुहोस्, हात कम्मरमा।"** | Stand tall · Clear space ahead |
| 14–26s | Step one foot forward and lower the back knee toward the floor — front shin fairly vertical, front knee over the mid-foot | **"एउटा पैतला अगाडि सारेर पछाडिको घुँडालाई भुइँतिर झार्नुहोस्। अगाडिको नली प्रायः ठाडो, घुँडा पैतलामाथि।"** | Step forward · Lower the back knee |
| 26–38s | Push through the front foot and step the back foot forward into the next lunge on the other leg | **"अगाडिको पैतलाको बलले पछाडिको खुट्टालाई अगाडि ल्याएर अर्को खुट्टामा lunge गर्नुहोस्।"** | Push through the front foot · Step into the next lunge |
| 38–52s | Continue slowly, staying tall between steps | **"बिस्तारै अगाडि बढ्दै जानुहोस्, हरेक पाइलाबीच सिधा।"** | Continue slowly · Tall between steps |
| 52–56s | Stop, stand tall | **"रोक्नुहोस्, सिधा उभिनुहोस्।"** | Stop · Stand tall |
| 56–60s | Safety screen | **"घुँडा भित्र खुम्चियो, दुख्यो, वा लर्खरायो भने Reverse Lunge (#2360) मा फर्कनुहोस्।"** | Knee caving, painful, or giving way? Go back to reverse lunges |

**Don't include:** long, lurching steps or the front knee caving in — controlled steps, knee tracking over the foot.
**Modification:** do reverse lunges (#2360) in place first — they're easier to control than walking forward.
**Safety line:** घुँडामा तीखो दुखाइ वा लर्खराएमा रोक्नुहोस्।
