# Low Back Pain (Lumbar) — Exercise Video Scripts

**Batch 2: Intermediate (Level 2).** The 22 intermediate-level Lumbar
exercises in the app (`Spine → Lumbar`, difficulty 2). One script per
exercise, mapped to its `ExerciseMain` record by ID.

Format and rules are the same as [`lumbar-beginner.md`](lumbar-beginner.md):
4-column table (`Time` · `Visual` · `Voice-over` Nepali · `On-screen text`),
then **Don't include** · **Modification** · **Safety line**.
**No dosage in the video** (it's on the exercise record + the physio's
prescription). **No disease claims.**

Finished video → upload to the host → paste the URL into that exercise's
`hosted_video_url` field (admin → Exercise main → the matching ID).

## 1. Dead Bug (Modified – Heel slides) · app exercise #2272

**Target: 55–65 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, arms toward ceiling, one heel sliding out | **"यो Dead Bug व्यायाम हो। यसले हात-खुट्टा चलाउँदा पनि कम्मरलाई स्थिर राख्न सिकाउँछ।"** | Dead Bug (heel slides) |
| 5–14s | Hook-lying, both arms straight up toward the ceiling | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्। दुवै हात माथितिर सिधा उठाउनुहोस्।"** | Knees bent · Arms straight up |
| 14–22s | Gentle abdominal draw-in, low back settles to neutral | **"नाभिलाई हल्का भित्रतिर तान्नुहोस्, कम्मर भुइँमा स्थिर।"** | Gently brace · Back stays still |
| 22–34s | One heel slides slowly away as the opposite arm reaches back overhead | **"अब एउटा कुर्कुच्चा बिस्तारै अगाडि घिसार्नुहोस्, साथसाथै विपरीत हात टाउकोमाथि पछाडि लैजानुहोस्।"** | One heel out · Opposite arm back |
| 34–43s | Return the arm and heel to the start, back never arches | **"बिस्तारै हात र खुट्टा फर्काउनुहोस्। कम्मर उही ठाउँमा।"** | Return slowly · Back doesn't lift |
| 43–56s | Other side, same | **"अब अर्को तर्फ यही चाल गर्नुहोस्।"** | Now the other side |
| 56–62s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि हात-खुट्टा झारेर आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest when done |
| 62–65s | Safety screen | **"कम्मर उठ्न थाल्यो भने चाल सानो बनाउनुहोस्।"** | Back lifting? Make it smaller |

**Don't include:** letting the low back pop up off the floor as the limbs move — cut the range instead.
**Modification:** too hard — move only the leg (arms stay up), add the opposite arm later.
**Safety line:** कम्मर उठ्न थाल्यो भने चाल सानो बनाउनुहोस्।

## 2. Lumbar Stabilization with Ball (Dead bug) · app exercise #2292

**Target: 55–65 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, holding a ball between one hand and the opposite knee | **"यो बल सहितको Dead Bug हो। बलले हात र घुँडाको जोडलाई सक्रिय राख्न मद्दत गर्छ।"** | Dead Bug with ball |
| 5–15s | Hook-lying, both hips and knees bent to 90°, ball pressed between both hands and both knees | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, घुँडा ९० डिग्रीमा माथि। हात र घुँडाको बीचमा बल हल्का थिच्नुहोस्।"** | Hips + knees at 90° · Press the ball |
| 15–23s | Gentle abdominal set | **"नाभिलाई हल्का भित्रतिर तान्नुहोस्, कम्मर स्थिर।"** | Gently brace |
| 23–35s | Extend one arm and the opposite leg away, still pressing the ball with the other hand/knee | **"एउटा हात र विपरीत खुट्टा बिस्तारै अगाडि फैलाउनुहोस्। अर्को हात-घुँडाले बल थिचिरहनुहोस्।"** | Extend opposite arm + leg · Keep pressing |
| 35–44s | Return to the start | **"बिस्तारै फर्काउनुहोस्।"** | Return slowly |
| 44–56s | Other side | **"अब अर्को तर्फ गर्नुहोस्।"** | Now the other side |
| 56–62s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest |
| 62–65s | Safety screen | **"कम्मर उठे वा दुखे रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if the back lifts or hurts |

**Don't include:** dropping the extended arm/leg all the way to the floor — they stop just above the floor.
**Modification:** no ball — a rolled towel or small pillow works, or do it without (that's exercise #2272).
**Safety line:** कम्मर उठे वा दुखे रोक्नुहोस्।

## 3. Supine Bridge · app exercise #2273

**Target: 50–60 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist shows the finished bridge (straight line shoulder–hip–knee) | **"यो Bridge व्यायाम हो। यसले नितम्ब र तिघ्राका मांसपेशीलाई काम गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Supine Bridge |
| 5–13s | Hook-lying, feet flat hip-width, arms by the sides | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्। घुँडा खुम्च्याएर पैतला भुइँमा, हात छेउमा।"** | Knees bent · Feet flat · Arms by sides |
| 13–22s | Gentle brace, then lift the hips to a straight shoulder-to-knee line | **"पेट हल्का कस्दै नितम्बलाई बिस्तारै माथि उठाउनुहोस्, काँधदेखि घुँडासम्म सिधा नभएसम्म।"** | Lift to a straight line — shoulder to knee |
| 22–31s | Hold, hips level, breathing, low back not over-arched | **"नितम्ब तेर्सो राख्नुहोस्, सास फेरिरहनुहोस्। कम्मर बढी नझुकाउनुहोस्।"** | Hold · Don't over-arch |
| 31–40s | Lower slowly, one part of the back at a time | **"अब बिस्तारै, ढाडको एक-एक भाग गर्दै तल झार्नुहोस्।"** | Lower slowly |
| 40–52s | Repeat with control, side view | **"यही चाल नियन्त्रणसहित दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat with control |
| 52–58s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि खुट्टा सिधा गरेर आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest when done |
| 58–60s | Safety screen | **"कम्मरमा दुखाइ बढेमा वा तिघ्रामा ऐंठन आएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if back pain increases or hamstring cramps |

**Don't include:** pushing the hips as high as possible — stop at a straight line, not beyond.
**Modification:** hamstring cramp — move the feet slightly further from the hips, or lift less.
**Safety line:** कम्मरमा दुखाइ बढेमा वा तिघ्रामा ऐंठन आएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 4. Single Leg Bridge · app exercise #2274

**Target: 50–60 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist in a bridge with one foot lifted, hips level | **"यो Single Leg Bridge हो। यो Bridge को अलि गाह्रो रूप हो।"** | Single Leg Bridge |
| 5–13s | Hook-lying setup | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्, पैतला भुइँमा।"** | Knees bent · Feet flat |
| 13–20s | Lift the hips into a normal bridge first | **"पहिले सामान्य Bridge जस्तै नितम्ब माथि उठाउनुहोस्।"** | Lift into a bridge |
| 20–31s | Straighten one leg / lift one foot a few cm — hips stay perfectly level, no drop to one side | **"अब एउटा पैतला भुइँबाट हल्का उठाउनुहोस्। नितम्ब एकातिर नझुकोस्, तेर्सै राख्नुहोस्।"** | Lift one foot · Hips stay level |
| 31–39s | Hold briefly, breathing | **"छोटो समय समात्नुहोस्, सास फेरिरहनुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Keep breathing |
| 39–46s | Return the foot, lower the hips with control | **"पैतला फर्काउनुहोस्, अनि बिस्तारै नितम्ब तल झार्नुहोस्।"** | Return · Lower slowly |
| 46–56s | Other leg | **"अब अर्को खुट्टामा गर्नुहोस्।"** | Now the other leg |
| 56–60s | Safety screen | **"नितम्ब एकातिर झुक्न थाल्यो भने दुई खुट्टाकै Bridge मा फर्कनुहोस्।"** | Hips dropping? Go back to the two-leg bridge |

**Don't include:** letting the pelvis tip toward the lifted-leg side — if it can't stay level, this exercise is too soon.
**Modification:** keep the lifted knee bent and just take the toes off the floor, rather than fully straightening the leg.
**Safety line:** नितम्ब एकातिर झुक्न थाल्यो भने दुई खुट्टाकै Bridge मा फर्कनुहोस्।

## 5. Supine Figure 4 Stretch (Piriformis) · app exercise #2280

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, one ankle crossed over the opposite knee, thigh pulled in | **"यो Figure 4 Stretch हो। यसले नितम्ब गहिराइका मांसपेशीमा हल्का तनाव दिन सक्छ।"** | Figure 4 Stretch (piriformis) |
| 5–13s | Hook-lying, then cross one ankle over the opposite thigh, just above the knee | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्। एउटा गोलीगाँठोलाई अर्को घुँडाभन्दा माथि तिघ्रामा राख्नुहोस्।"** | Cross the ankle over the opposite thigh |
| 13–25s | Reach through and hold behind the supporting thigh, gently draw it toward the chest | **"तल्लो खुट्टाको तिघ्रा पछाडिबाट समातेर बिस्तारै छातीतिर तान्नुहोस्।"** | Hold behind the thigh · Draw toward the chest |
| 25–36s | Hold, mild stretch deep in the buttock, breathing, low back stays down | **"नितम्बमा हल्का तनाव महसुस गर्नुहोस्। सास फेरिरहनुहोस्, कम्मर भुइँमै।"** | Mild stretch in the buttock — not sharp |
| 36–44s | Release, switch sides | **"बिस्तारै छोड्नुहोस्, अनि अर्को तर्फ गर्नुहोस्।"** | Release · Then the other side |
| 44–50s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest |
| 50–55s | Safety screen | **"खुट्टामा झमझमाहट वा तीखो दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if tingling or sharp pain |

**Don't include:** forcing the thigh in hard or bouncing — a gentle, steady pull.
**Modification:** can't reach the thigh — loop a towel behind it, or do the seated version (#2281).
**Safety line:** खुट्टामा झमझमाहट वा तीखो दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 6. Side-Lying Leg Lift (Abduction) · app exercise #2278

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist side-lying, top leg lifting straight up | **"यो Side-Lying Leg Lift हो। यसले नितम्ब छेउका मांसपेशीलाई काम गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Side-Lying Leg Lift |
| 5–15s | Lying on one side, body in a straight line, bottom knee slightly bent for balance, head supported | **"एक छेउ ढल्किनुहोस्। शरीर सिधा, तल्लो घुँडा सन्तुलनका लागि हल्का खुम्च्याउनुहोस्, टाउको हातमा।"** | Lie on your side · Body in a straight line |
| 15–20s | Gentle abdominal set, pelvis vertical (not rolling back) | **"पेट हल्का कस्नुहोस्, श्रोणी सिधा — पछाडि नबटारियोस्।"** | Brace gently · Don't roll the pelvis back |
| 20–31s | Lift the top leg straight up a comfortable height, toes pointing forward | **"माथिको खुट्टालाई सिधा राखेर बिस्तारै आरामदायी उचाइसम्म उठाउनुहोस्, औँला अगाडितिर।"** | Lift the top leg — toes point forward |
| 31–39s | Lower slowly with control | **"बिस्तारै तल झार्नुहोस्।"** | Lower slowly |
| 39–48s | Repeat, then roll over and do the other side | **"दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्, अनि अर्को छेउ फर्केर गर्नुहोस्।"** | Repeat · Then the other side |
| 48–55s | Safety screen | **"कम्मर वा नितम्बमा दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if back or hip pain increases |

**Don't include:** rolling the pelvis backward and swinging the leg forward — that swaps to the wrong muscle; keep the toes pointing straight ahead.
**Modification:** lift lower, or bend the top knee slightly if a straight leg is too heavy.
**Safety line:** कम्मर वा नितम्बमा दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 7. Clamshell · app exercise #2279

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist side-lying, knees bent, top knee opening like a clam | **"यो Clamshell व्यायाम हो। यसले नितम्ब छेउका मांसपेशीलाई काम गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Clamshell |
| 5–15s | Side-lying, both knees bent ~45°, feet together and in line with the back, hips stacked | **"एक छेउ ढल्किनुहोस्। दुवै घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्, पैतला जोडेर राख्नुहोस्, श्रोणी सिधा।"** | Knees bent · Feet together · Hips stacked |
| 15–26s | Keeping the feet touching, lift the top knee open — pelvis does NOT roll back | **"पैतला जोडिएकै राखेर माथिको घुँडालाई बिस्तारै माथि खोल्नुहोस्। श्रोणी नबटारियोस्।"** | Open the top knee · Feet stay together · Pelvis still |
| 26–34s | Hold briefly, then lower with control | **"छोटो समय समात्नुहोस्, अनि बिस्तारै तल ल्याउनुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Lower slowly |
| 34–45s | Repeat, then the other side | **"दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्, अनि अर्को छेउ गर्नुहोस्।"** | Repeat · Then the other side |
| 45–55s | Safety screen | **"कम्मर वा नितम्बमा दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if back or hip pain increases |

**Don't include:** rocking the whole pelvis backward to open the knee higher — the range is small, the pelvis stays still.
**Modification:** put a hand on the top hip to feel it staying still; open the knee less.
**Safety line:** कम्मर वा नितम्बमा दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 8. Side Plank (Knees down) · app exercise #2289

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist in a knees-down side plank, body in a straight line | **"यो Side Plank हो — घुँडा टेकाएको सजिलो रूप। यसले कम्मरको छेउका मांसपेशीलाई काम गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Side Plank (knees down) |
| 5–15s | On one side, forearm under the shoulder, knees bent behind, top hand on the hip | **"एक छेउ ढल्किनुहोस्। कुहिनो काँधमुनि, घुँडा पछाडि खुम्च्याउनुहोस्, माथिको हात कम्मरमा।"** | Elbow under shoulder · Knees bent behind |
| 15–26s | Lift the hips so the body is a straight line from head to knees | **"नितम्बलाई माथि उठाउनुहोस्, टाउकोदेखि घुँडासम्म सिधा लाइन बनाउनुहोस्।"** | Lift hips — straight line, head to knees |
| 26–34s | Hold, breathing, hips not sagging or piking | **"यो स्थिति समात्नुहोस्, सास फेरिरहनुहोस्। नितम्ब नझर्नुहोस्, नउचाल्नुहोस्।"** | Hold · Keep breathing · Hips don't sag |
| 34–41s | Lower with control | **"बिस्तारै नितम्ब तल झार्नुहोस्।"** | Lower slowly |
| 41–50s | Other side | **"अब अर्को छेउ गर्नुहोस्।"** | Now the other side |
| 50–55s | Safety screen | **"कम्मर वा काँधमा दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if back or shoulder pain increases |

**Don't include:** letting the hips drop toward the floor — come down and rest instead of holding a sagging position.
**Modification:** shorten the hold; or keep the hips a little lower so the line is head-to-hip rather than head-to-knee.
**Safety line:** कम्मर वा काँधमा दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 9. Bird Dog (Quadruped) · app exercise #2275

**Target: 60–70 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist shows the finished position | **"यो Bird Dog व्यायाम हो। यसले ढाड र पेटका मांसपेशीलाई सन्तुलनसहित काम गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Bird Dog |
| 5–14s | Hands and knees, flat back | **"चार हातखुट्टामा आउनुहोस्। हात काँधमुनि, घुँडा नितम्बमुनि। ढाड सिधा।"** | Hands under shoulders · Flat back |

| 31–40s | Return to centre, rotate the other way | **"बीचमा फर्कनुहोस्, अनि अर्को तर्फ घुमाउनुहोस्।"** | Return · Then the other way |
| 40–48s | Return to centre, rest | **"बीचमा फर्केर आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Return to centre |
| 48–50s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy or painful |

**Don't include:** swinging the arms hard to twist further — the movement is slow and controlled, small range.
**Modification:** do the seated version (#2286) if standing balance is a concern.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 20. Standing Hip Hinge (Stick on back) · app exercise #2290

**Target: 55–65 seconds.** Teaches bending from the hips with a neutral spine — the pattern used for lifting.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist standing, a stick along the spine, hinging forward | **"यो Hip Hinge हो। यसले कम्मर होइन, नितम्बबाट अगाडि झुक्न सिकाउँछ।"** | Standing Hip Hinge |
| 6–18s | Standing, feet hip-width. A stick/dowel held vertically against the back — touching the head, upper back, and tailbone | **"पछाडि एउटा लौरो राख्नुहोस् — टाउको, माथिल्लो ढाड र पुच्छर, तीनवटै ठाउँमा छुनुपर्छ।"** | Stick touches head · upper back · tailbone |
| 18–30s | Push the hips backward and let the chest tip forward — all three contact points stay touching the stick, knees soft | **"नितम्बलाई पछाडि धकेल्दै छातीलाई अगाडि झुकाउनुहोस्। तीनवटै ठाउँ लौरोमा टाँसिएकै रहनुपर्छ, घुँडा हल्का खुकुलो।"** | Push hips back · Keep all three points touching |
| 30–38s | Stop when the back is about to lose contact, then stand back up by driving the hips forward | **"लौरोबाट छुट्न लाग्दा रोकेर, नितम्ब अगाडि धकेल्दै सिधा उभिनुहोस्।"** | Stop before the back rounds · Stand up hips-first |
| 38–52s | Repeat slowly, side view | **"यही चाल बिस्तारै दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat slowly |
| 52–60s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest when done |
| 60–65s | Safety screen | **"कम्मर गोलो हुन थाल्यो भने कम झुक्नुहोस्।"** | Back rounding? Hinge less far |

**Don't include:** squatting straight down (bending the knees a lot) — this is a hip movement, the knees only soften slightly.
**Modification:** no stick — a broom handle works; or do it with the hips a hand's width from a wall and aim to touch the wall with the hips.
**Safety line:** कम्मर गोलो हुन थाल्यो भने कम झुक्नुहोस्।

## 21. Wall Sit (Lumbar neutral) · app exercise #2287

**Target: 40–50 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist in a wall sit, back flat against the wall | **"यो Wall Sit व्यायाम हो। यसले तिघ्रा र कम्मरका मांसपेशीलाई काम गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Wall Sit |
| 5–14s | Standing with the back against a wall, feet about a foot forward, hip-width | **"भित्तामा ढाड टेकाएर उभिनुहोस्, पैतला अगाडि करिब एक फुट, कम्मर जत्तिको चौडा।"** | Back on the wall · Feet a step forward |
| 14–24s | Slide down the wall until the hips and knees are near 90°, low back kept flat against the wall | **"भित्तामा घिस्रिँदै तल झर्नुहोस्, जबसम्म घुँडा करिब ९० डिग्री नहुन्छ। कम्मर भित्तामा टाँसिएको।"** | Slide down to ~90° · Low back flat on the wall |
| 24–33s | Hold, breathing, knees over the ankles (not past the toes) | **"यो स्थिति समात्नुहोस्, सास फेरिरहनुहोस्। घुँडा गोलीगाँठोमाथि, औँलाभन्दा अगाडि नजाओस्।"** | Hold · Keep breathing |
| 33–41s | Slide back up to standing | **"भित्तामा घिस्रिँदै फेरि माथि उभिनुहोस्।"** | Slide back up |
| 41–48s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest when done |
| 48–50s | Safety screen | **"घुँडा वा कम्मरमा दुखाइ बढेमा माथि आउनुहोस् र रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Come up and stop if knee or back pain increases |

**Don't include:** sliding so low that the knees go well past 90° — that loads the knees; stop at about 90°, higher if the knees complain.
**Modification:** don't go as low — a shallow wall sit still works; a gym ball behind the back makes sliding smoother.
**Safety line:** घुँडा वा कम्मरमा दुखाइ बढेमा माथि आउनुहोस् र रोक्नुहोस्।

## 22. Squat (Bodyweight – Lumbar neutral) · app exercise #2291

**Target: 50–60 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist doing a controlled bodyweight squat, back neutral | **"यो Bodyweight Squat हो। यसले खुट्टा र नितम्बका मांसपेशीलाई काम गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Bodyweight Squat |
| 5–14s | Standing, feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out, arms forward for balance | **"खुट्टा काँध जत्तिको चौडा, औँला हल्का बाहिरतिर। सन्तुलनका लागि हात अगाडि।"** | Feet shoulder-width · Toes slightly out |
| 14–26s | Push the hips back and bend the knees, lowering as far as the back stays neutral — chest stays up | **"नितम्बलाई पछाडि धकेल्दै घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्, जति तल जाँदा कम्मर सिधा रहन्छ त्यति मात्र। छाती माथि।"** | Hips back · Down as far as the back stays neutral |
| 26–34s | Brief pause at the bottom, knees tracking over the toes, weight through the mid-foot | **"तल पुगेर छोटो समय रोक्नुहोस्। घुँडा औँलातिरै, तौल पैतलाको बीचमा।"** | Knees track over the toes |
| 34–43s | Stand back up, driving through the feet, hips and chest rising together | **"पैतलाको बलले सिधा उभिनुहोस्, नितम्ब र छाती सँगै माथि।"** | Stand up · Hips and chest rise together |
| 43–54s | Repeat with control, side view | **"यही चाल नियन्त्रणसहित दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat with control |
| 54–60s | Safety screen | **"तल पुग्दा कम्मर गोलो भयो भने त्यति तल नजानुहोस्।"** | Back rounding at the bottom? Don't go as low |

**Don't include:** going deep enough that the pelvis tucks and the low back rounds at the bottom ("butt wink") — stop above that point.
**Modification:** squat down onto a chair or box and stand back up, so there's a target and support.
**Safety line:** तल पुग्दा कम्मर गोलो भयो भने त्यति तल नजानुहोस्।
