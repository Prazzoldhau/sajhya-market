# Knee — Exercise Video Scripts

**Batch 1: Beginner (Level 1).** The 29 beginner-level Knee exercises in
the app (`Lower Limb → Knee`, difficulty 1), mapped to their
`ExerciseMain` records by ID.

Format and rules match the other batches: 4-column table
(`Time` · `Visual` · `Voice-over` Nepali · `On-screen text`), then
**Don't include** · **Modification** · **Safety line**.
**No dosage in the video.** **No disease claims.**

**Standard knee safety line for the whole batch:**

> **घुँडा धेरै सुन्निएमा, तीखो दुखाइ भएमा, वा घुँडा लर्खराउन थालेमा रोक्नुहोस्।**
> *(Stop if the knee swells a lot, if there's sharp pain, or if it starts to give way.)*

Many of these are gentle range-of-motion work for a stiff or post-op
knee — the physio prescribes which direction (bending vs straightening)
and how far.

Finished video → host → `hosted_video_url` on the matching ID.

## 1. Quadriceps Set (Static Quad Contraction) · app exercise #3044

**Target: 35–45 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist long-sitting, leg straight, tightening the thigh so the kneecap slides up | **"यो Quad Set व्यायाम हो। यसले तिघ्राको अगाडिको मांसपेशीलाई सक्रिय गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ। घुँडा चल्दैन।"** | Quadriceps Set |
| 5–13s | Sitting or lying with the leg straight, a small rolled towel under the ankle (heel just off the floor) | **"खुट्टा सिधा राखेर बस्नुहोस् वा पल्टनुहोस्। गोलीगाँठोमुनि सानो बेरेको तौलिया राख्नुहोस्।"** | Leg straight · Small towel under the ankle |
| 13–25s | Tighten the thigh muscle, pushing the back of the knee down toward the floor — the kneecap pulls up, nothing else moves | **"तिघ्राको मांसपेशी कस्नुहोस्, घुँडाको पछाडिलाई भुइँतिर थिच्दै। घुँडाको बिर्को माथि तानिन्छ, अरू केही चल्दैन।"** | Tighten the thigh · Press the back of the knee down |
| 25–33s | Hold, breathing normally | **"समात्नुहोस्, सामान्य सास फेरिरहनुहोस्।"** | Hold · Keep breathing |
| 33–40s | Fully release, repeat | **"पूरै छोड्नुहोस्, अनि दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Fully relax · Repeat |
| 40–45s | Safety screen | **"घुँडा वरिपरि तीखो दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if sharp pain around the knee |

**Don't include:** lifting the leg or bending the knee — it's a still contraction only.
**Modification:** press the back of the knee down onto a rolled towel to feel it better.
**Safety line:** घुँडामा तीखो दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 2. Seated Knee Extension Isometric · app exercise #2372

**Target: 35–45 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist sitting with the leg extended out straight, holding a quad contraction | **"यो बसेर गरिने Quad Isometric हो। घुँडा चल्दैन।"** | Seated Knee Extension Isometric |
| 5–13s | Sitting with the leg out straight in front (heel on a low stool), or long-sitting on the floor | **"खुट्टालाई अगाडि सिधा फैलाएर बस्नुहोस्, कुर्कुच्चा सानो स्टुलमा।"** | Leg straight out in front |
| 13–25s | Contract the thigh to push the knee straight and firm — hold, no movement at the joint | **"तिघ्राको मांसपेशी कसेर घुँडालाई सिधा र कडा बनाउनुहोस् — समात्नुहोस्, जोर्नी नचलोस्।"** | Contract the thigh · Knee stays still |
| 25–33s | Hold, breathing | **"समात्नुहोस्, सास फेरिरहनुहोस्।"** | Hold · Keep breathing |
| 33–40s | Release, repeat | **"छोड्नुहोस्, दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Release · Repeat |
| 40–45s | Safety screen | **"घुँडामा तीखो दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if sharp pain in the knee |

**Don't include:** actually lifting or straightening the leg from a bent position — the knee is already straight; this is a hold.
**Modification:** do the quad set lying down (#3044) if sitting is uncomfortable.
**Safety line:** घुँडामा तीखो दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 3. Straight Leg Raise · app exercise #2352

**Target: 40–50 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, one knee bent, lifting the other straight leg to the height of the bent knee | **"यो Straight Leg Raise हो। यसले तिघ्रा र घुँडा वरिपरिका मांसपेशीलाई काम गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Straight Leg Raise |
| 5–14s | Lying face-up, one knee bent with the foot flat, the exercising leg straight | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्। एउटा घुँडा खुम्च्याएर पैतला भुइँमा, अर्को खुट्टा सिधा।"** | One knee bent · Other leg straight |
| 14–22s | First, set the quad (tighten the thigh, lock the knee straight) | **"पहिले तिघ्राको मांसपेशी कसेर घुँडा सिधा बनाउनुहोस्।"** | First: tighten the thigh, lock the knee |
| 22–32s | Keeping the knee locked straight, lift the whole leg to about the height of the bent knee | **"घुँडा सिधा राखेर पूरै खुट्टालाई अर्को घुँडाको उचाइसम्म बिस्तारै उठाउनुहोस्।"** | Lift to the height of the bent knee · Keep the knee straight |
| 32–42s | Lower slowly with control, repeat | **"बिस्तारै तल झार्नुहोस्, अनि दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Lower slowly · Repeat |
| 42–50s | Safety screen | **"घुँडा खुम्चिन थाल्यो वा कम्मरमा दुख्न थाल्यो भने खुट्टा कम उठाउनुहोस्।"** | Knee bending or back hurting? Lift lower |

**Don't include:** the knee bending as the leg lifts, or the low back arching — set the quad first and lift only as high as the knee stays locked.
**Modification:** smaller lift; hold the quad set with no lift at all first.
**Safety line:** कम्मर वा घुँडामा दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 4. Glute Bridge · app exercise #2351

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, lifting the hips to a straight shoulder–hip–knee line | **"यो Bridge व्यायाम हो। यसले नितम्ब र तिघ्राको पछाडिका मांसपेशीलाई काम गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Glute Bridge |
| 5–13s | Hook-lying, feet flat hip-width, arms by the sides | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्, पैतला भुइँमा।"** | Knees bent · Feet flat |
| 13–24s | Squeeze the glutes and lift the hips to a straight line from shoulder to knee | **"चाक कस्दै नितम्बलाई बिस्तारै माथि उठाउनुहोस्, काँधदेखि घुँडासम्म सिधा।"** | Lift to a straight line — shoulder to knee |
| 24–32s | Hold, hips level, breathing | **"नितम्ब तेर्सो राख्नुहोस्, सास फेरिरहनुहोस्।"** | Hold · Keep breathing |
| 32–42s | Lower slowly, repeat | **"बिस्तारै तल झार्नुहोस्, दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Lower slowly · Repeat |
| 42–50s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest when done |
| 50–55s | Safety screen | **"घुँडा वा कम्मरमा दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if knee or back pain increases |

**Don't include:** over-arching the low back to lift higher — stop at a straight line.
**Modification:** smaller lift; feet a little further from the hips if the hamstrings cramp.
**Safety line:** घुँडा वा कम्मरमा दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 5. Ankle Pumps · app exercise #3056

**Target: 30–40 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, leg supported, moving the foot up and down at the ankle | **"यो Ankle Pump हो। यसले रक्तसञ्चार राख्न र खुट्टा जकडिनबाट बचाउन मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Ankle Pumps |
| 5–12s | Lying or sitting with the leg out straight and supported | **"खुट्टा सिधा फैलाएर, सहारामा राख्नुहोस्।"** | Leg straight and supported |
| 12–24s | Point the foot down, then pull it up toward the shin — a full, easy range, repeated steadily | **"पैतलालाई तल देखाउनुहोस्, अनि नलीतिर तान्नुहोस् — पूरा, सहज दूरीमा, लगातार।"** | Point down · Pull up · Steady, full range |
| 24–34s | Continue at a comfortable pace | **"आरामदायी गतिमा दोहोर्‍याइरहनुहोस्।"** | Keep going at a comfortable pace |
| 34–40s | Safety screen | **"पिँडुलामा तीखो दुखाइ, रातोपन वा असामान्य सुन्निने भएमा physiotherapist लाई भन्नुहोस्।"** | Tell your physio if the calf is sharply painful, red, or unusually swollen |

**Don't include:** — (nothing to warn against for this one; it's very gentle).
**Modification:** do it as often through the day as the physio advises, especially after surgery or long rest.
**Safety line:** पिँडुलामा तीखो दुखाइ, रातोपन वा असामान्य सुन्निने भएमा physiotherapist लाई भन्नुहोस्।

## 6. Hip AROM in Supine · app exercise #3057

**Target: 45–55 seconds.** Keeps the hip moving while the knee recovers.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, moving one leg through gentle hip bending, out-to-the-side, and small rolls | **"यो अभ्यासले घुँडा निको हुँदै गर्दा नितम्बको जोर्नी चलायमान राख्न मद्दत गर्छ।"** | Hip Active ROM (supine) |
| 5–13s | Lying face-up, legs straight | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, खुट्टा सिधा।"** | Lie face-up · Legs straight |
| 13–24s | Slide/bend the hip to bring the knee up a little (heel sliding along the floor), then lower | **"कुर्कुच्चा भुइँमा घिसार्दै एउटा घुँडा थोरै माथि ल्याउनुहोस्, अनि तल।"** | Bend the hip a little · Heel slides · Then lower |
| 24–34s | Slide the straight leg out to the side and back | **"सिधा खुट्टालाई छेउतिर घिसारेर फर्काउनुहोस्।"** | Slide the leg out to the side and back |
| 34–44s | Gently roll the whole leg in and out (kneecap toward and away from the midline) | **"पूरै खुट्टालाई भित्र-बाहिर हल्का घुमाउनुहोस्।"** | Roll the leg gently in and out |
| 44–52s | Other leg, same, within comfort | **"अर्को खुट्टामा पनि आरामभित्र गर्नुहोस्।"** | Other leg · Within comfort |
| 52–55s | Safety screen | **"घुँडा वा नितम्बमा तीखो दुखाइ भएमा दूरी घटाउनुहोस्।"** | Reduce the range if sharp pain in the knee or hip |

**Don't include:** forcing the knee to bend far — the knee stays comfortable; this is about the hip.
**Modification:** smaller ranges; skip the movements that pull on the knee.
**Safety line:** घुँडा वा नितम्बमा तीखो दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 7. Supine Knee Flexion with Towel · app exercise #2366

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, sliding the heel toward the buttock along the floor on a towel | **"यो घुँडा खुम्च्याउने ROM अभ्यास हो, तौलियाको सहायताले।"** | Knee Flexion with Towel |
| 5–13s | Lying face-up, a small towel under the foot on a smooth floor, leg straight | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्। पैतलामुनि सानो तौलिया, चिल्लो भुइँमा। खुट्टा सिधा।"** | Towel under the foot · Smooth floor |
| 13–25s | Slide the heel toward the buttock, bending the knee as far as it comfortably goes | **"कुर्कुच्चालाई चाकतिर घिसार्दै घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्, जति आरामले जान्छ त्यति।"** | Slide the heel toward the buttock · Comfortable range |
| 25–33s | Hold briefly at the end, then slide the heel back to straight | **"अन्तिममा छोटो समय रोकेर, कुर्कुच्चा फेरि पछाडि घिसारेर घुँडा सिधा बनाउनुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Then slide back to straight |
| 33–44s | Repeat slowly | **"यही चाल बिस्तारै दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat slowly |
| 44–50s | Safety screen | **"घुँडा धेरै सुन्निएमा वा तीखो दुखाइ भएमा दूरी घटाउनुहोस्।"** | Reduce the range if the knee swells or sharp pain |

**Don't include:** forcing past a firm block of pain — a gentle stretch feeling at the end is fine, sharp pain is not.
**Modification:** use a strap around the foot to help pull the heel in (#3047); or do it with the opposite foot pushing the ankle.
**Safety line:** घुँडा धेरै सुन्निएमा वा तीखो दुखाइ भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 9. Passive Knee Flexion Stretch (Heel Slide) · app exercise #3038

**Target: 40–50 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, using the other leg (ankle hooked behind) to gently push the operated leg's heel toward the buttock | **"यो अर्को खुट्टाको सहायताले घुँडा खुम्च्याउने passive stretch हो।"** | Passive Knee Flexion (heel slide) |
| 5–14s | Lying face-up, the exercising leg's foot flat, the other leg's ankle hooked behind that ankle | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्। जुन खुट्टाको अभ्यास हो, त्यसको पछाडि अर्को खुट्टाको गोलीगाँठो अड्काउनुहोस्।"** | Hook the other ankle behind |
| 14–26s | The good leg gently pushes the exercising foot back, bending that knee — the exercising leg stays passive and relaxed | **"राम्रो खुट्टाले अभ्यास गर्ने पैतलालाई बिस्तारै पछाडि धकेल्छ, घुँडा खुम्च्याउँदै। अभ्यास गर्ने खुट्टा खुकुलो।"** | Good leg does the pushing · Operated leg stays relaxed |
| 26–34s | Hold at a comfortable end range, breathing, then release | **"आरामदायी अन्तिम बिन्दुमा समात्नुहोस्, सास फेर्नुहोस्, अनि छोड्नुहोस्।"** | Hold at a comfortable end range · Then release |
| 34–44s | Repeat slowly | **"यही चाल बिस्तारै दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat slowly |
| 44–50s | Safety screen | **"तीखो दुखाइ भएमा दबाब घटाउनुहोस्।"** | Ease off if sharp pain |
