# Neck (Cervical) — Exercise Video Scripts

**Batch 1: Beginner (Level 1).** The 20 beginner-level Cervical exercises
in the app (`Spine → Cervical`, difficulty 1), mapped to their
`ExerciseMain` records by ID.

Other batches: [`cervical-intermediate.md`](cervical-intermediate.md) (Level 2, 19) ·
[`cervical-advanced.md`](cervical-advanced.md) (Level 3, 17 — 12 self-guided, 5 reference).

Format and rules match the Lumbar batches
([`lumbar-beginner.md`](lumbar-beginner.md)): 4-column table
(`Time` · `Visual` · `Voice-over` Nepali · `On-screen text`), then
**Don't include** · **Modification** · **Safety line**.
**No dosage in the video.** **No disease claims.**

**Neck-specific:** every movement is *small, slow and gentle* — the videos
must show it that way, never a big or fast range. The standard safety line
for this whole batch is:

> **रिंगटा लाग्यो, वा हात-पाखुरामा झमझमाहट फैलियो भने तुरुन्तै रोक्नुहोस्।**
> *(Stop at once if you feel dizzy, or if tingling spreads into the arm.)*

Individual scripts add their own extra note where relevant.

Finished video → host → paste URL into that exercise's `hosted_video_url`
(admin → Exercise main → matching ID).

## 1. Chin Tuck (Supine) · app exercise #2744

**Target: 40–50 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, gently making a "double chin" | **"यो Chin Tuck व्यायाम हो। यसले घाँटीको गहिराइका मांसपेशीलाई सक्रिय गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Chin Tuck (supine) |
| 5–13s | Lying face-up, knees bent, head resting flat (a thin towel under the head if needed) | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्। टाउको भुइँमा सिधा राख्नुहोस्।"** | Lie face-up · Head resting flat |
| 13–24s | Gently nod the chin down and slightly back, lengthening the back of the neck — a small "double chin" | **"चिउँडोलाई बिस्तारै तल र हल्का पछाडि तान्नुहोस्, घाँटीको पछाडि लामो हुँदै। दोहोरो चिउँडो जस्तो।"** | Gently tuck the chin · Lengthen the back of the neck |
| 24–32s | Hold, breathing, no lifting the head off the floor | **"यो स्थिति समात्नुहोस्, सास फेरिरहनुहोस्। टाउको भुइँबाट नउठाउनुहोस्।"** | Hold · Head stays on the floor |
| 32–40s | Release slowly to neutral | **"अब बिस्तारै छोड्नुहोस्।"** | Release slowly |
| 40–47s | Repeat, side view | **"यही चाल बिस्तारै दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat gently |
| 47–50s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy or arm tingling |

**Don't include:** lifting the head, or jamming the chin down hard — it's a small, gentle glide.
**Modification:** a thin folded towel under the head if lying flat tips the chin up.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 2. Deep Cervical Flexor Isometric (Supine) · app exercise #2756

**Target: 40–50 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, gently pressing the back of the head into the floor | **"यो व्यायामले घाँटीका गहिरा मांसपेशीलाई हल्का सक्रिय गर्छ। टाउको चल्दैन।"** | Deep Neck Flexor Isometric |
| 5–13s | Lying face-up, knees bent, head flat, chin gently tucked first | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्। पहिले चिउँडो हल्का भित्रतिर तान्नुहोस्।"** | Lie face-up · Gently tuck the chin first |
| 13–25s | Very gently press the back of the head straight down into the floor — about 20% effort, no movement | **"अब टाउकोको पछाडिलाई भुइँमा एकदम हल्का थिच्नुहोस् — थोरै मात्र बल, टाउको नचलाउनुहोस्।"** | Gently press the head down — about 20% effort |
| 25–33s | Hold, breathing normally | **"समात्नुहोस्, सामान्य सास फेरिरहनुहोस्। सास नरोक्नुहोस्।"** | Hold · Keep breathing |
| 33–41s | Fully release | **"अब पूरै खुकुलो छोड्नुहोस्।"** | Fully relax |
| 41–47s | Repeat | **"यही चाल दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat |
| 47–50s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy or arm tingling |

**Don't include:** pressing hard, or lifting/tilting the head — it's a light, still contraction.
**Modification:** if pressing into the floor is uncomfortable, press the head lightly back into a folded towel instead.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 3. Suboccipital Release (Supine with towel) · app exercise #2755

**Target: 35–45 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, a small rolled towel under the base of the skull, head relaxed | **"यो घाँटीको माथिल्लो भागलाई आराम दिने अभ्यास हो।"** | Suboccipital Release (with towel) |
| 5–15s | Lying face-up, knees bent, a thin rolled towel placed just under the base of the skull (not the neck) | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्। पातलो बेरेको तौलियालाई टाउकोको फेदमा राख्नुहोस्, घाँटीमा होइन।"** | Thin rolled towel under the base of the skull |
| 15–28s | Let the head sink and fully relax onto the towel, jaw loose, breathing slow | **"टाउकोलाई तौलियामा बिस्तारै छाड्नुहोस्, पूरै खुकुलो। बङ्गारा खुकुलो, सास ढिलो।"** | Let the head sink · Jaw loose · Breathe slowly |
| 28–38s | Stay, side view | **"यही स्थितिमा केही मिनेट रहनुहोस्।"** | Stay a few minutes |
| 38–42s | Slowly remove the towel and rest | **"बिस्तारै तौलिया हटाएर आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Remove the towel · Rest |
| 42–45s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy or arm tingling |

**Don't include:** a thick roll that forces the head back into extension — the towel is thin, the head stays close to neutral.
**Modification:** even thinner towel, or a soft small ball, if a firm roll feels like too much pressure.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 4. Cervical Flexion (Nodding) · app exercise #2748

**Target: 35–45 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist seated, gently nodding the chin toward the chest and back | **"यो घाँटी अगाडि निहुराउने चाल हो।"** | Cervical Flexion (nodding) |
| 5–13s | Sitting tall on a firm chair, shoulders relaxed, gaze forward | **"बलियो कुर्सीमा सिधा बस्नुहोस्, काँध खुकुलो, हेराइ अगाडि।"** | Sit tall · Shoulders relaxed |
| 13–24s | Slowly nod the chin toward the chest, only to a comfortable point | **"चिउँडोलाई बिस्तारै छातीतिर निहुराउनुहोस्, जति आरामदायी हुन्छ त्यति मात्र।"** | Nod the chin toward the chest — comfortable range only |
| 24–31s | Brief pause, breathing | **"छोटो समय रोकेर सास फेर्नुहोस्।"** | Brief pause · Keep breathing |
| 31–40s | Return slowly to neutral, repeat | **"बिस्तारै सिधा स्थितिमा फर्कनुहोस्। दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Return slowly · Repeat |
| 40–45s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy or arm tingling |

**Don't include:** pulling the head down with the hands, or dropping it fast — the neck muscles do the work, slowly.
**Modification:** smaller range; combine with a gentle chin tuck first if a plain nod feels pinchy at the back.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

| 0–5s | Therapist seated, gently lifting the chin toward the ceiling and back | **"यो घाँटी पछाडि ढल्काउने चाल हो — माथितिर हेर्दै।"** | Cervical Extension (looking up) |
| 5–13s | Sitting tall, shoulders relaxed | **"बलियो कुर्सीमा सिधा बस्नुहोस्, काँध खुकुलो।"** | Sit tall · Shoulders relaxed |
| 13–24s | Slowly lift the chin toward the ceiling, small comfortable range, mouth relaxed | **"चिउँडोलाई बिस्तारै आकाशतिर उठाउनुहोस्, सानो आरामदायी दूरीमा मात्र। मुख खुकुलो।"** | Lift the chin gently — small range |
| 24–31s | Brief pause, breathing, then return | **"छोटो समय रोकेर, बिस्तारै सिधा फर्कनुहोस्।"** | Brief pause · Return slowly |
| 31–40s | Repeat, side view | **"यही चाल बिस्तारै दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat gently |

**Safety line:** रिंगटा, धमिलो देखिने वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 6. Cervical Lateral Flexion (Ear to shoulder) · app exercise #2750

**Target: 40–50 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist seated, tilting one ear toward the shoulder, shoulder staying down | **"यो कानलाई काँधतिर झुकाउने चाल हो।"** | Ear to Shoulder |
| 5–13s | Sitting tall, both shoulders relaxed and down | **"बलियो कुर्सीमा सिधा बस्नुहोस्, दुवै काँध खुकुलो र तल।"** | Sit tall · Shoulders down |
| 13–24s | Slowly tilt one ear toward that shoulder — the shoulder does NOT come up to meet it | **"बिस्तारै एउटा कानलाई काँधतिर झुकाउनुहोस्। काँध माथि नउठाउनुहोस्।"** | Ear toward shoulder · Shoulder stays down |
| 24–31s | Brief pause at a comfortable point, breathing | **"आरामदायी ठाउँमा छोटो समय रोक्नुहोस्, सास फेर्नुहोस्।"** | Brief pause · Keep breathing |
| 31–40s | Return to centre, then the other side | **"बीचमा फर्कनुहोस्, अनि अर्को तर्फ गर्नुहोस्।"** | Return to centre · Then the other side |
| 40–47s | Repeat both sides slowly | **"दुवै तर्फ बिस्तारै दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat both sides slowly |
| 47–50s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy or arm tingling |

**Don't include:** shrugging the shoulder up toward the ear, or forcing the tilt with a hand — that's a stretch (#2759), not this one.
**Modification:** sit on the hand of the side you're tilting away from, to keep that shoulder down.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 8. Chin Tuck (Seated) · app exercise #2745

**Target: 35–45 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist seated, gently drawing the chin/head straight back | **"यो बसेर गर्ने Chin Tuck हो।"** | Chin Tuck (seated) |
| 5–13s | Sitting tall on a firm chair, gaze forward, shoulders relaxed | **"बलियो कुर्सीमा सिधा बस्नुहोस्, हेराइ अगाडि, काँध खुकुलो।"** | Sit tall · Eyes forward |
| 13–24s | Draw the whole head straight back over the shoulders (not tilting) — a small "double chin", eyes stay level | **"टाउकोलाई सिधा पछाडि, काँधमाथि लैजानुहोस् — निहुराउने होइन। दोहोरो चिउँडो जस्तो, आँखा तेर्सो।"** | Glide the head straight back · Eyes stay level |
| 24–32s | Hold, breathing | **"समात्नुहोस्, सास फेरिरहनुहोस्।"** | Hold · Keep breathing |
| 32–40s | Release forward to neutral, repeat | **"बिस्तारै अगाडि सामान्य स्थितिमा फर्कनुहोस्। दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Release · Repeat |
| 40–45s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy or arm tingling |

**Modification:** a finger lightly on the chin as a target to move away from; smaller range.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 10. Chin Tuck with Overpressure (Finger assist) · app exercise #2757

**Target: 40–50 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist doing a chin tuck, then adding a light finger press on the chin | **"यो Chin Tuck मा औँलाले हल्का थप दबाब दिने अभ्यास हो।"** | Chin Tuck + gentle overpressure |
| 5–14s | Sitting tall, perform a normal seated chin tuck first | **"बलियो कुर्सीमा सिधा बस्नुहोस्। पहिले सामान्य Chin Tuck गर्नुहोस्।"** | Sit tall · Do a normal chin tuck first |
| 14–26s | At the end of the tuck, place two fingers on the chin and press straight back very gently — a small extra bit | **"तान्ने काम सकिएपछि, दुई औँला चिउँडोमा राखेर सिधा पछाडि एकदम हल्का थिच्नुहोस् — थोरै मात्र थप।"** | Two fingers on the chin · Press straight back, very gently |
