# Neck (Cervical) — Exercise Video Scripts

**Batch 2: Intermediate (Level 2).** The 19 intermediate-level Cervical
exercises in the app (`Spine → Cervical`, difficulty 2), mapped to their
`ExerciseMain` records by ID.

Other batches: [`cervical-beginner.md`](cervical-beginner.md) ·
[`cervical-advanced.md`](cervical-advanced.md).

Format and rules match the earlier batches: 4-column table
(`Time` · `Visual` · `Voice-over` Nepali · `On-screen text`), then
**Don't include** · **Modification** · **Safety line**.
**No dosage in the video.** **No disease claims.**

**Standard neck safety line for the whole batch:**

> **रिंगटा लाग्यो, वा हात-पाखुरामा झमझमाहट फैलियो भने तुरुन्तै रोक्नुहोस्।**
> *(Stop at once if you feel dizzy, or if tingling spreads into the arm.)*

**Two exercises need a clinic device.** #2776 and #2777 use a pressure
biofeedback cuff (Stabilizer / PBU). The physio inflates it and sets the
target pressure; the video only shows the movement. Note this on screen —
most patients won't own the cuff and shouldn't improvise one.

Finished video → host → paste URL into that exercise's `hosted_video_url`
(admin → Exercise main → matching ID).

## 1. Deep Neck Flexor Endurance (Supine) · app exercise #2764

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, chin tucked, head barely off the floor, holding | **"यो घाँटीका गहिरा मांसपेशीको सहनशीलता बढाउने अभ्यास हो।"** | Deep Neck Flexor Endurance |
| 5–14s | Lying face-up, knees bent, head resting flat, perform a gentle chin tuck first | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्। पहिले चिउँडो हल्का भित्रतिर तान्नुहोस्।"** | Lie face-up · Gentle chin tuck first |
| 14–26s | Keeping the tuck, lift the head just a hair off the floor — the chin does NOT poke up | **"तान कायम राख्दै टाउकोलाई भुइँबाट एकदम थोरै उठाउनुहोस्। चिउँडो माथि नउठोस्।"** | Lift the head a hair · Keep the chin tucked |
| 26–36s | Hold at that low height, breathing normally | **"त्यही सानो उचाइमा समात्नुहोस्, सामान्य सास फेरिरहनुहोस्।"** | Hold · Keep breathing |
| 36–45s | Lower the head fully and rest | **"बिस्तारै टाउको पूरै तल राखेर आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Lower fully · Rest |
| 45–52s | Repeat | **"यही चाल दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat |
| 52–55s | Safety screen | **"चिउँडो अगाडि निस्कन थाल्यो वा घाँटी काम्न थाल्यो भने रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if the chin pokes forward or the neck shakes |

**Don't include:** lifting the head high — the height is tiny; the challenge is the tuck, not the lift.
**Modification:** don't lift at all — just hold the chin tuck against the floor (that's #2756), and add the small lift later.
**Safety line:** चिउँडो अगाडि निस्कन थाल्यो वा घाँटी काम्न थाल्यो भने रोक्नुहोस्।

## 2. Chin Tuck with Head Lift (Supine) · app exercise #2765

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, tucking the chin then lifting the head an inch or two, holding, lowering | **"यो Chin Tuck सँगै टाउको उठाउने अभ्यास हो।"** | Chin Tuck with Head Lift |
| 5–14s | Lying face-up, knees bent, head flat | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्, टाउको भुइँमा।"** | Lie face-up · Head flat |
| 14–22s | Perform a firm chin tuck | **"चिउँडोलाई भित्रतिर तान्नुहोस्।"** | Tuck the chin |
| 22–32s | Keeping the tuck, lift the head 1–2 inches off the floor — the tuck holds the whole time | **"तान कायम राख्दै टाउकोलाई भुइँबाट एक-दुई इन्च उठाउनुहोस्। तान नछुटोस्।"** | Lift 1–2 inches · The tuck stays |
| 32–40s | Brief hold, then lower with control — release the tuck only after the head is down | **"छोटो समय समात्नुहोस्, अनि बिस्तारै तल झार्नुहोस्। टाउको भुइँमा पुगेपछि मात्र तान छोड्नुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Lower with control |
| 40–50s | Repeat | **"यही चाल दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat |
| 50–55s | Safety screen | **"चिउँडो अगाडि निस्क्यो, घाँटी काम्यो, वा रिंगटा भयो भने रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if the chin pokes, the neck shakes, or you feel dizzy |

**Don't include:** letting the chin lead the lift (head tips back) — the tuck comes first and stays.
**Modification:** lift less; or do the endurance version (#2764) with a barely-there lift.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 3. Deep Cervical Flexor Progression (Pressure Biofeedback) · app exercise #2776

**Target: 55–65 seconds.** Needs a pressure biofeedback cuff — a clinic device.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist supine, an inflated pressure cuff under the neck, watching the gauge while gently tucking the chin | **"यो अभ्यासमा घाँटीमुनि दबाब-नाप्ने यन्त्र राखिन्छ। physiotherapist ले दबाब मिलाउनुहुन्छ।"** | Needs a pressure biofeedback cuff (clinic) |
| 6–18s | Lying face-up, knees bent, the folded cuff placed under the curve of the neck and inflated to the physio's starting number | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्। यन्त्रलाई घाँटीको बाङ्गोमुनि राखेर physiotherapist ले फुलाउनुहुन्छ।"** | Cuff under the neck curve · Physio sets the start |
| 18–32s | Perform a slow, gentle chin tuck to nudge the gauge up by the target amount — a small, precise movement | **"बिस्तारै चिउँडो तान्दै यन्त्रको नापलाई तोकिएको मात्रामा बढाउनुहोस् — सानो, नियन्त्रित चाल।"** | Slow chin tuck to the target number |
| 32–42s | Hold at that pressure, breathing, gauge steady | **"त्यही नापमा समात्नुहोस्, सास फेरिरहनुहोस्। नाप स्थिर राख्नुहोस्।"** | Hold at the target · Keep the gauge steady |
| 42–52s | Release slowly back to the start number, rest | **"बिस्तारै सुरुको नापमा फर्कनुहोस्, आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Release to the start · Rest |
| 52–60s | Repeat | **"यही चाल दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat |
| 60–65s | Safety screen | **"चिउँडो अगाडि निस्क्यो वा नाप उफ्रियो भने चाल सानो बनाउनुहोस्।"** | Chin poking, or the gauge jumping? Make it smaller |

**Don't include:** improvising a cuff from a blood-pressure sleeve without the physio setting it up — the target numbers are prescribed.
**Modification:** without the cuff, do the plain deep-flexor isometric (#2756) or endurance hold (#2764).
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 4. Craniocervical Flexion Test (Hold) · app exercise #2777

**Target: 55–65 seconds.** Same cuff; graded holds at increasing pressures.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist supine with the cuff, holding a gentle chin tuck at one gauge level, then a slightly deeper one | **"यो पहिलेकै यन्त्रसँग गरिने अभ्यास हो — बढ्दो नापमा एक-एक तह समात्ने।"** | Craniocervical Flexion — graded holds (cuff) |
| 6–16s | Supine setup, cuff under the neck, inflated to the start number by the physio | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्। यन्त्रलाई घाँटीमुनि राखेर physiotherapist ले सुरुको नाप मिलाउनुहुन्छ।"** | Cuff under the neck · Physio sets the start |
| 16–30s | Gentle chin tuck to the first target level, hold steady for a few breaths | **"बिस्तारै चिउँडो तान्दै पहिलो नापसम्म पुग्नुहोस्, केही सासभर स्थिर समात्नुहोस्।"** | Tuck to level 1 · Hold steady |
| 30–44s | Release to the start, then tuck a small bit further to the next level, hold again | **"सुरुमा फर्कनुहोस्, अनि थोरै बढी तानेर अर्को नापमा पुगेर फेरि समात्नुहोस्।"** | Return · Then to the next level · Hold |
| 44–56s | Only progress to a level where the tuck stays smooth — no chin poke, no shake | **"जहाँसम्म चिउँडो नराम्ररी नउठिकन र नकाम्पिकन जान्छ, त्यहीँसम्म मात्र बढाउनुहोस्।"** | Only progress while the tuck stays smooth |
| 56–62s | Return to the start, rest | **"सुरुको नापमा फर्केर आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Return to the start · Rest |
| 62–65s | Safety screen | **"चिउँडो निस्क्यो वा घाँटी काम्यो भने त्यही तहमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Chin poking or neck shaking? Stop at that level |

**Don't include:** pushing to the top pressure just to reach it — the deep flexors must do the work cleanly at each level.
**Modification:** stay at one comfortable level and build the hold time (that's #2776) before adding levels.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 5. Prone Chin Tuck (Face down) · app exercise #2766

**Target: 40–50 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist face down, forehead on a folded towel, gently tucking the chin and lifting the head a small way | **"यो घोप्टो परेर गरिने Chin Tuck हो — गुरुत्वविरुद्ध।"** | Prone Chin Tuck (face down) |
| 5–14s | Lying face down, forehead resting on a folded towel, arms by the sides, gaze at the floor | **"घोप्टो पल्टनुहोस्। निधार बेरेको तौलियामा, हात छेउमा, हेराइ भुइँतिर।"** | Lie face down · Forehead on a towel |
| 14–26s | Tuck the chin (lengthen the back of the neck), then lift the forehead just off the towel — eyes stay pointing down | **"चिउँडो तान्नुहोस्, अनि निधारलाई तौलियाबाट एकदम थोरै उठाउनुहोस्। आँखा भुइँतिरै।"** | Tuck · Lift the forehead a little · Eyes down |
| 26–34s | Brief hold, neck long, then lower | **"छोटो समय समात्नुहोस्, घाँटी लामो। अनि बिस्तारै तल राख्नुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Then lower |
| 34–43s | Repeat | **"यही चाल दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat |
| 43–50s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy or arm tingling |

**Don't include:** looking up as the head lifts — that's extension; here the gaze stays down and the chin stays tucked.
**Modification:** do it lying with the head over the edge of the bed so there's less to lift; or do the supine version (#2765).
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

| 50–55s | — | — | — |

**Safety line:** रिंगटा, धमिलो देखिने वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 7. Prone Cervical Extension with Chin Tuck · app exercise #2768

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist face down, lifting the head, then adding a chin tuck at the top | **"यो अघिको अभ्यासमै माथि पुगेपछि Chin Tuck थप्ने हो।"** | Prone Extension + Chin Tuck |

| 14–24s | Lift the head a small way (gaze down), as in #2767 | **"टाउकोलाई बिस्तारै थोरै उठाउनुहोस्, आँखा भुइँतिर।"** | Lift a small way · Eyes down |
| 24–33s | At the top, draw the chin in (lengthen the back of the neck) without lowering the head | **"माथि पुगेपछि टाउको नझारी चिउँडो भित्रतिर तान्नुहोस्, घाँटीको पछाडि लामो हुँदै।"** | At the top: tuck the chin · Head stays up |
| 33–41s | Brief hold, then release the tuck and lower with control | **"छोटो समय समात्नुहोस्, अनि तान छोड्दै बिस्तारै तल झार्नुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Then lower |
| 41–50s | Repeat | **"यही चाल दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat |
| 50–55s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy or arm tingling |

**Don't include:** cranking the head higher when adding the tuck — the head height stays the same, only the chin moves.
**Modification:** master plain prone extension (#2767) first; add the tuck once that's easy.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 8. Side-Lying Cervical Lateral Flexion · app exercise #2769

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist on one side, lifting the head straight up toward the ceiling, neck in line with the spine | **"यो एक छेउ ढल्किएर टाउको माथि उठाउने अभ्यास हो — घाँटीको छेउका मांसपेशीका लागि।"** | Side-Lying Cervical Lateral Flexion |
| 5–15s | Lying on one side, body in a straight line, head resting on a low pillow or the lower arm, so the neck is level with the spine | **"एक छेउ ढल्किनुहोस्, शरीर सिधा। टाउकोलाई सानो तकिया वा हातमा राख्नुहोस्, घाँटी मेरुदण्डसँग सिधा।"** | Lie on your side · Neck level with the spine |
| 15–27s | Lift the head straight up toward the ceiling a small way — do not tuck, do not turn, just a pure sideways lift | **"टाउकोलाई सिधा आकाशतिर बिस्तारै थोरै उठाउनुहोस् — न तान्ने, न घुमाउने, सिधा छेउतिर मात्र।"** | Lift straight up · Don't tuck or turn |
| 27–34s | Brief hold, then lower with control | **"छोटो समय समात्नुहोस्, अनि बिस्तारै तल राख्नुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Lower with control |
| 34–46s | Repeat, then roll over and do the other side | **"दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्, अनि अर्को छेउ फर्केर गर्नुहोस्।"** | Repeat · Then the other side |
| 46–55s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy or arm tingling |

**Don't include:** a big lift or letting the head rotate/tuck — a small, pure sideways movement.
**Modification:** lift barely off the support and build from there; a slightly higher pillow makes the start easier.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 9. Quadruped Cervical Retraction · app exercise #2770

**Target: 40–50 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 46–54s | Repeat to the other side, and with a nod up/down | **"अर्को तर्फ, अनि माथि-तल पनि यही अभ्यास गर्नुहोस्।"** | Repeat other side · And up/down |
| 54–60s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy or arm tingling |

**Don't include:** fast turns or peeking — slow movement, eyes genuinely closed until the check.
**Modification:** smaller turn range; do it lying down if sitting with eyes closed feels unsteady.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 19. Cervical Proprioception (Laser pointer) · app exercise #2782

**Target: 55–65 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist seated wearing a headband with a small laser pointer, tracing a shape drawn on the wall | **"यो अभ्यासमा टाउकोमा सानो लेजर बाँधेर भित्तामा बनाइएको आकार पछ्याइन्छ।"** | Laser Pointer Tracking |
| 6–18s | Sitting tall about an arm's length from a wall, a light headband holding a small laser pointing at the wall; a simple shape (circle, or a line, or the letter shapes) marked on the wall at eye level | **"भित्ताबाट करिब एक हात टाढा सिधा बस्नुहोस्। टाउकोमा लेजर बाँध्नुहोस्, भित्तामा आँखाको उचाइमा एउटा सरल आकार बनाउनुहोस्।"** | Sit an arm's length from the wall · Laser on a headband · Shape at eye level |

**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।
