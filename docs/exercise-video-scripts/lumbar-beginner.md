# Low Back Pain (Lumbar) — Exercise Video Scripts

**Batch 1: Beginner (Level 1).** The 15 beginner-level Lumbar exercises
already in the app (`Spine → Lumbar`, difficulty 1). One script per
exercise, each mapped to its exact `ExerciseMain` record by ID.

Other batches in this folder:
[`lumbar-intermediate.md`](lumbar-intermediate.md) (Level 2, 22 exercises).
Advanced (Level 3) — pending a decision on the loaded barbell/kettlebell
lifts (see below).

---

## How to use this file

**Each script is a table:** `Time` · `Visual` (what the camera shows) ·
`Voice-over` (Nepali, spoken) · `On-screen text` (short English/number
cue shown on screen). Then three notes: **Don't include** · **Modification**
· **Safety line**.

**Two fixed rules, every script:**

1. **No dosage in the video.** Sets / reps / hold time already live on
   each exercise record (`default_sets`, `default_reps`, `hold_time_sec`)
   and the physio overrides them per patient in the prescription. The
   video only teaches *how*.
2. **No disease claims.** "may gently move / gently work the back, hips
   and thighs" — never "treats back pain".

**Flexion vs extension:** some of these bend the spine forward (Child's
Pose, Knee-to-Chest, Seated Flexion), some backward (Standing Lumbar
Extension). That's fine — the physio prescribes whichever direction
suits the patient. The videos don't need to warn about it.

**Where the finished video goes:** upload to the video host, paste the
public URL into that exercise's `hosted_video_url` field (admin →
Exercise main → the row with the matching ID below).

## 1. Supine Hook Lying (Neutral spine) · app exercise #2264

**Target: 35–45 seconds.** Teaches the neutral resting position most of
the other exercises start from.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist relaxed in hook-lying | **"यो Hook Lying स्थिति हो — कम्मरका धेरै व्यायाम यहीँबाट सुरु हुन्छन्।"** | Hook Lying — neutral spine |
| 5–14s | Face-up, both knees bent ~90°, feet flat hip-width, arms loose by sides | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्। दुवै घुँडा खुम्च्याएर पैतला भुइँमा, कम्मर जत्तिको चौडा राख्नुहोस्। हात छेउमा खुकुलो।"** | Knees bent · Feet hip-width · Arms relaxed |
| 14–24s | Close-up of the low back — small natural gap, neither forced flat nor arched | **"कम्मर र भुइँको बीचमा सानो प्राकृतिक खाली ठाउँ हुन्छ। यसलाई जबरजस्ती थिच्नु पर्दैन, न बढी उठाउनु।"** | Keep the small natural curve — don't force it |
| 24–34s | Whole body settling, easy breathing | **"काँध, चाक र खुट्टा खुकुलो छोड्नुहोस्। सामान्य रूपमा सास फेर्नुहोस्।"** | Relax · Breathe normally |
| 34–42s | Hold, side view | **"यही आरामदायी स्थितिमा केही क्षण रहनुहोस्।"** | Rest here |
| 42–45s | Safety screen | **"कम्मर तानिएको महसुस भए घुँडामुनि तकिया राख्नुहोस्।"** | Tight? Support under the knees |

**Don't include:** pressing the back hard into the floor — that's the Pelvic Tilt, a different exercise; this keeps the natural curve.
**Modification:** a rolled towel or pillow under the knees if lying flat feels tight.
**Safety line:** कम्मर तानिएको महसुस भए घुँडामुनि तकिया राख्नुहोस्।

## 2. Diaphragmatic Breathing (Supine) · app exercise #2258

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, one hand on chest, one on belly | **"यो पेटले सास फेर्ने व्यायाम हो। यसले शरीरलाई आराम दिन र पेट भित्रका मांसपेशी सक्रिय गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Diaphragmatic Breathing |
| 5–13s | Hook-lying, one hand on chest, one on belly | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्। एउटा हात छातीमा, अर्को पेटमा राख्नुहोस्।"** | One hand on chest · One on belly |
| 13–25s | Slow inhale through the nose — belly rises, chest stays quiet (close-up of hands) | **"नाकबाट बिस्तारै सास तान्नुहोस्। पेटको हात माथि उठ्नुपर्छ, छातीको हात प्रायः स्थिर।"** | Inhale — belly rises, chest stays still |
| 25–37s | Slow exhale through the mouth — belly falls, gentle navel draw-in | **"मुखबाट बिस्तारै सास छोड्नुहोस्। पेट तल जान्छ, नाभि हल्का भित्रतिर।"** | Exhale slowly — belly falls |
| 37–47s | Continue, calm rhythm, side view | **"यही लयमा शान्त भएर सास फेरिरहनुहोस्। हतार नगर्नुहोस्।"** | Slow, calm rhythm |
| 47–53s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि सामान्य सास फेर्दै आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest when done |
| 53–55s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा लागेमा सामान्य सास फेर्नुहोस्।"** | Dizzy? Return to normal breathing |

**Don't include:** big forceful breaths or shoulder shrugging — the movement is soft and low, into the belly.
**Modification:** the same breathing can be done sitting upright in a chair if lying is uncomfortable.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा लागेमा सामान्य सास फेर्नुहोस्।

## 3. Transverse Abdominis Draw-In · app exercise #2266

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, subtle draw-in | **"यो Abdominal Draw-In व्यायाम हो। यसले पेट भित्रका गहिरा मांसपेशीलाई सक्रिय गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Abdominal Draw-In |
| 5–13s | Hook-lying, fingers resting just inside the hip bones | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्। औँलाहरू कम्मरका हड्डीभित्र पेटमा राख्नुहोस्।"** | Fingers just inside the hip bones |
| 13–24s | Gentle navel-toward-spine draw; pelvis and back do NOT move | **"बिस्तारै नाभिलाई ढाडतिर तान्नुहोस्, मानौँ पेटलाई भित्रतिर खुम्च्याउँदै। श्रोणी र कम्मर नचलाउनुहोस्।"** | Draw navel in — pelvis stays still |
| 24–34s | Hold, breathing continues normally | **"हल्का तनाव कायम राख्दै सामान्य सास फेरिरहनुहोस्। सास नरोक्नुहोस्।"** | Hold gently · Keep breathing |
| 34–42s | Release | **"अब बिस्तारै छोड्नुहोस्।"** | Release slowly |
| 42–50s | Repeat; side view shows the back staying neutral | **"यही चाल बिस्तारै दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्। कम्मर उही ठाउँमा।"** | Repeat · Back doesn't move |
| 50–55s | Safety screen | **"दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if pain increases |

**Don't include:** sucking the stomach in hard or holding the breath — about 30% effort, breathing continues.
**Modification:** hard to feel it — try on hands and knees, letting the belly relax down, then gently lifting it.
**Safety line:** दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 4. Supine Pelvic Tilt · app exercise #2257

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | Therapist shows the finished position (low back flat) | **"यो Pelvic Tilt व्यायाम हो। यसले कम्मर वरिपरिका मांसपेशीलाई हल्का सक्रिय गर्न मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Pelvic Tilt |
| 4–12s | Hook-lying setup | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्। दुवै घुँडा खुम्च्याएर पैतला भुइँमा राख्नुहोस्।"** | Lie face-up · Knees bent · Feet flat |
| 12–20s | Hand in the natural gap under the low back | **"अहिले कम्मर र भुइँको बीचमा सानो खाली ठाउँ छ।"** | Small gap under the back |
| 20–30s | Close-up: pelvis tilts, low back presses gently down | **"बिस्तारै पेट भित्रतिर तान्दै कम्मरलाई भुइँतर्फ हल्का थिच्नुहोस्। खाली ठाउँ बन्द हुन्छ।"** | Gently flatten the back down |
| 30–38s | Hold, breathing | **"यो स्थिति समात्नुहोस्, सामान्य सास फेरिरहनुहोस्। सास नरोक्नुहोस्।"** | Hold · Keep breathing |
| 38–45s | Release to neutral | **"अब बिस्तारै छोड्नुहोस्, कम्मरलाई सामान्य स्थितिमा फर्काउनुहोस्।"** | Slowly release |
| 45–52s | Repeat, side view | **"यही चाल बिस्तारै दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat gently |
| 52–55s | Safety screen | **"दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if pain increases |

**Don't include:** "जोडले थिच्नुहोस्" (press hard) — small, gentle movement.
**Modification:** a thin pillow under the head is fine.
**Safety line:** दुखाइ बढेमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 5. Pelvic Clock (Supine) · app exercise #2268

**Target: 50–60 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist supine, small circular pelvic movement | **"यो Pelvic Clock व्यायाम हो। यसले श्रोणीलाई सबै दिशामा बिस्तारै चलाउन मद्दत गर्न सक्छ।"** | Pelvic Clock |
| 6–14s | Hook-lying setup | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्। कल्पना गर्नुहोस् — श्रोणीमा एउटा घडी छ।"** | Imagine a clock face on your pelvis |
| 14–24s | Tilt to 12 (low back flattens) then 6 (low back gently arches) | **"पहिले १२ बजेतिर — कम्मर हल्का भुइँमा। अनि ६ बजेतिर — कम्मर हल्का माथि।"** | 12 o'clock ↔ 6 o'clock |
| 24–34s | Tilt to 3 (one hip) then 9 (other hip) | **"अब ३ बजेतिर — एकातिरको कम्मर हल्का माथि। अनि ९ बजेतिर — अर्कोतिर।"** | 3 o'clock ↔ 9 o'clock |
| 34–46s | Smooth small circle joining all points, both directions | **"अब सबै बिन्दु जोडेर बिस्तारै सानो गोलो घुमाउनुहोस्, दुवै दिशा।"** | Small smooth circles — both directions |
| 46–54s | Return to the middle, rest | **"श्रोणीलाई बीचको सामान्य स्थितिमा ल्याएर आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Return to the middle |
| 54–60s | Safety screen | **"कुनै दिशामा दुखे त्यो दिशा छोड्नुहोस्।"** | Skip any direction that hurts |

**Don't include:** big or fast movements — the circle is small (a few centimetres) and slow.
**Modification:** if the full circle is confusing, just do 12↔6 (that's the Pelvic Tilt) and add the sides later.
**Safety line:** कुनै दिशामा दुखे त्यो दिशा छोड्नुहोस्।

## 7. Supine Heel Slides · app exercise #2265

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist slides one heel out and back | **"यो Heel Slide व्यायाम हो। यसले खुट्टा चलाउँदा पनि कम्मरलाई स्थिर राख्न सिकाउँछ।"** | Heel Slides |
| 5–13s | Hook-lying setup | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, दुवै घुँडा खुम्च्याउनुहोस्, पैतला भुइँमा।"** | Knees bent · Feet flat |
| 13–20s | Gentle abdominal draw-in first | **"पहिले नाभिलाई हल्का भित्रतिर तान्नुहोस्, कम्मर स्थिर।"** | Gently brace · Back stays still |
| 20–31s | One heel slides slowly away until the leg is nearly straight; heel stays on the floor | **"अब एउटा कुर्कुच्चा बिस्तारै अगाडि घिसार्नुहोस्, जबसम्म खुट्टा प्रायः सिधा नहुन्छ। कुर्कुच्चा भुइँमै।"** | Slide heel away · Keep it on the floor |
| 31–40s | Slide the heel back to the start; the back stays flat throughout | **"बिस्तारै कुर्कुच्चा फेरि पछाडि ल्याउनुहोस्। कम्मर उही ठाउँमा।"** | Slide back · Back doesn't arch |
| 40–50s | Other leg, same | **"अब अर्को खुट्टामा यही चाल गर्नुहोस्।"** | Now the other leg |
| 50–55s | Safety screen | **"कम्मर उठ्न थाल्यो भने चाल सानो बनाउनुहोस्।"** | Back lifting? Make the slide smaller |

**Don't include:** letting the low back arch off the floor as the leg straightens — reduce the range instead.
**Modification:** a sock on the heel, or a smooth floor, if the heel drags.
**Safety line:** कम्मर उठ्न थाल्यो भने चाल सानो बनाउनुहोस्।
