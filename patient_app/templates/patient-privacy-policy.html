Privacy Policy

Sajhya Patient App · Last updated {{ last_updated }}

This policy explains what the Sajhya patient app collects, why, and what control you have over it. Sajhya is used by physiotherapy patients to view exercises prescribed by their physiotherapist, give feedback on them, and order therapy products.

Health information. Sajhya handles information about your health, including your diagnosis and prescribed exercises. We treat this as sensitive personal data. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with advertisers or data brokers — ever.

1. What we collect

2. Camera

The app asks for camera access only when you choose to scan a QR code — to sign in, activate your account, or pair with a physiotherapist. Scanning happens entirely on your device. No photo or video is recorded, stored, or transmitted to us or anyone else. If you decline camera access you can still use every part of the app; you just sign in with your Patient Code and PIN instead.

3. What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking software.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photo library, microphone, or files.
  • No payment card details — marketplace orders are settled on delivery.

4. Who can see your information

  • Your physiotherapist and their clinic. The clinician treating you can see your profile, prescriptions, exercise feedback and orders, so they can manage your care.
  • Suppliers, for orders only. When you place a marketplace order, the delivery details needed to fulfil it are shared with the party dispatching it — nothing about your diagnosis or exercises.
  • Our hosting provider. Data is stored on servers operated on our behalf, under contract, solely to run Sajhya.
  • Where the law requires it. If compelled by a valid legal order.

We do not sell your information, and we never share health information for marketing.

5. How it is protected

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes and cannot be read by us.
  • The app blocks screenshots and screen recording on screens showing your diagnosis and prescriptions.
  • Your session is not backed up to cloud services or copied during device-to-device transfer.
  • Access by clinic staff is limited to the patients they are treating.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, in either of two ways:

  • In the app: Dashboard → ⋮ menu → Delete my account
  • On the web: {{ request.scheme }}://{{ request.get_host }}{% url 'patient-delete-account-web' %} — no app install needed

Deleted immediately and permanently:

  • Your name, contact number, password, QR code and sign-in token
  • Your pairing with your physiotherapist
  • Product recommendations made for you
  • Notification subscriptions — alerts to your devices stop at once
  • Delivery name, phone, address and notes on your past orders
  • Your shopping cart

You are signed out on every device and cannot sign in again.

Kept, in de-identified form:

Treatment records — prescriptions, exercise history, and your physiotherapist's session and visit notes — and the financial record of past orders are retained. Healthcare providers are required to keep clinical records, and businesses are required to keep records of sales.

These are kept without your name, contact details, password, or any other identifier. What remains is an anonymous Patient Code with no person attached to it. We do not attempt to re-identify de-identified records, and deleting your account cannot be undone — signing up again creates a new account with a new Patient Code.

7. How long we keep things

While your account is active, your information is kept so your physiotherapist can treat you. When you delete your account, personal data is erased as described in section 6. De-identified clinical and financial records are kept for as long as the treating practice's professional and statutory obligations require.

8. Children

Sajhya is intended for use by adults. Where a child is under a physiotherapist's care, the account is set up and managed by a parent, guardian, or the treating clinician on their behalf. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, or delete it. Deletion is available directly in the app and on the web (section 6); for access or correction, contact us and we will respond within a reasonable period. Corrections to clinical records are made through your treating physiotherapist, who is responsible for their accuracy.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where the change affects how your information is used, notify you in the app.
