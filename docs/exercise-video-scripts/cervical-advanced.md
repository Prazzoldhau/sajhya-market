# Neck (Cervical) — Exercise Video Scripts

**Batch 3: Advanced (Level 3).** The 17 advanced-level Cervical exercises
in the app (`Spine → Cervical`, difficulty 3), mapped to their
`ExerciseMain` records by ID.

Other batches: [`cervical-beginner.md`](cervical-beginner.md) ·
[`cervical-intermediate.md`](cervical-intermediate.md).

**Two sections** (same idea as `lumbar-advanced.md`):

- **A — self-guided full scripts** (12): loaded-with-light-weight holds,
  banded diagonal patterns, the vestibular / proprioception / perturbation
  drills, endurance holds, the breathing and wall variants. Some need a
  partner (marked).
- **B — reference only** (5): gym machines and specialist devices, plus
  two full-bodyweight neck-bridging moves that are **not filmed as
  instructional videos at all** — see below.

Standard neck safety line across both sections:

> **रिंगटा लाग्यो, वा हात-पाखुरामा झमझमाहट फैलियो भने तुरुन्तै रोक्नुहोस्।**

Finished video → host → `hosted_video_url` on the matching ID.

# Section A — Self-guided (full scripts)

## 1. Supine Deep Neck Flexor Hold with Limb Load · app exercise #2783

**Target: 50–60 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, holding a small chin-tuck head-lift while slowly moving the arms | **"यो अघिको Chin Tuck होल्डलाई हात-खुट्टा चलाएर अझ चुनौतीपूर्ण बनाउने अभ्यास हो।"** | Deep Neck Flexor Hold + limb load |
| 5–15s | Hook-lying, chin tucked, head lifted a hair off the floor, holding | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, चिउँडो तान्नुहोस्, टाउको भुइँबाट एकदम थोरै उठाएर समात्नुहोस्।"** | Chin tuck · Tiny head lift · Hold |
| 15–30s | Keeping the head steady and tucked, slowly raise one arm overhead and lower it, then the other | **"टाउको स्थिर र तानिएकै राखेर बिस्तारै एउटा हात टाउकोमाथि लैजानुहोस्, अनि तल। अनि अर्को हात।"** | Move the arms · Head stays perfectly still |
| 30–42s | Progress to sliding one heel away and back — head still does not move | **"अझ गाह्रो बनाउन एउटा कुर्कुच्चा अगाडि घिसारेर फर्काउनुहोस् — टाउको उही ठाउँमा।"** | Progress: slide a heel · Head still |
| 42–52s | Lower the head fully, rest | **"बिस्तारै टाउको पूरै तल राखेर आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Lower fully · Rest |
| 52–60s | Safety screen | **"चिउँडो अगाडि निस्क्यो, घाँटी काम्यो, वा रिंगटा भयो भने रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if the chin pokes, the neck shakes, or dizzy |

**Don't include:** the head bobbing or the chin poking as the limbs move — if it moves, drop the limb load.
**Modification:** hold the tuck with no head lift first, then add the lift, then the limbs.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 2. Prone Cervical Extension with Weight · app exercise #2784

**Target: 45–55 seconds.** Light weight only.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist face down, a light weight (small sandbag / light plate wrapped in a towel) resting on the back of the head, lifting a small way | **"यो घोप्टो Extension मै टाउकोको पछाडि हल्का तौल राखेर गरिन्छ। धेरै हल्का तौल मात्र।"** | Prone Cervical Extension + light weight |
| 5–15s | Lying face down, forehead on a folded towel, a light weight held/placed on the back of the head by hand or a partner | **"घोप्टो पल्टनुहोस्, निधार तौलियामा। टाउकोको पछाडि हल्का तौल राख्नुहोस्।"** | Face down · Light weight on the back of the head |
| 15–27s | Lift the head a small way using the back-of-neck muscles — gaze stays down, chin does not lead | **"घाँटीका पछाडिका मांसपेशीले टाउकोलाई बिस्तारै थोरै उठाउनुहोस्। आँखा भुइँतिरै।"** | Lift a small way · Eyes down |
| 27–35s | Brief hold, then lower with control | **"छोटो समय समात्नुहोस्, अनि बिस्तारै तल झार्नुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Lower with control |
| 35–45s | Repeat | **"यही चाल दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्।"** | Repeat |
| 45–55s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा, धमिलो देखिने वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा तौल हटाउनुहोस्।"** | Dizzy, blurred vision, or arm tingling? Remove the weight |

**Don't include:** any weight that makes the head drop or the range collapse — start with almost nothing.
**Modification:** no weight (that's #2767); progress the weight over weeks, not sessions.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा, धमिलो देखिने वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 3. Side-Lying Cervical Lateral Flexion with Weight · app exercise #2785

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist on one side, a light weight resting against the head, lifting the head straight up | **"यो अघिको Side-Lying अभ्यासमै टाउकोमा हल्का तौल राखेर गरिन्छ।"** | Side-Lying Lateral Flexion + light weight |
| 5–15s | Lying on one side, neck level with the spine, a light weight held against the upper side of the head | **"एक छेउ ढल्किनुहोस्, घाँटी मेरुदण्डसँग सिधा। टाउकोको माथिल्लो छेउमा हल्का तौल राख्नुहोस्।"** | Lie on your side · Light weight against the head |
| 15–27s | Lift the head straight up toward the ceiling a small way — no tuck, no turn | **"टाउकोलाई सिधा आकाशतिर बिस्तारै थोरै उठाउनुहोस् — न तान्ने, न घुमाउने।"** | Lift straight up · Small range |
| 27–35s | Brief hold, lower with control | **"छोटो समय समात्नुहोस्, अनि बिस्तारै तल राख्नुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Lower with control |
| 35–46s | Repeat, then the other side | **"दोहोर्‍याउनुहोस्, अनि अर्को छेउ गर्नुहोस्।"** | Repeat · Then the other side |
| 46–55s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा तौल हटाउनुहोस्।"** | Dizzy or arm tingling? Remove the weight |

**Don't include:** a heavy weight or a big lift — small movement, minimal load.
**Modification:** no weight (#2769) until that's easy for a full set.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 4. Theraband Cervical Pattern (Diagonal D1/D2) · app exercise #2786

**Target: 60–70 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist seated, band anchored diagonally, moving the head through a combined tuck-and-turn one way, then a lift-and-turn the other | **"यो ब्यान्ड सहितको छड्के चाल हो — तान्ने-घुमाउने र उठाउने-घुमाउने मिलाएर।"** | Theraband Diagonal Pattern (D1/D2) |
| 6–18s | Sitting tall, a light band around the head, anchored diagonally low on one side (e.g. down and to the left) | **"बलियो कुर्सीमा सिधा बस्नुहोस्। हल्का ब्यान्डलाई टाउको वरिपरि, एक छेउ तल छड्के अड्काउनुहोस्।"** | Band anchored diagonally low, one side |
| 18–32s | Move the head *away* from the anchor along the diagonal — chin tucks in and turns up-and-away at the same time, against the band | **"ब्यान्डविरुद्ध टाउकोलाई अड्काइएको ठाउँबाट पर, छड्के दिशामा लैजानुहोस् — चिउँडो तान्दै र माथि-पर घुमाउँदै एकैसाथ।"** | Move away from the anchor · Tuck + turn together |
| 32–42s | Return slowly along the same line, controlling the band | **"उही धर्सोमा बिस्तारै फर्कनुहोस्, ब्यान्ड नियन्त्रणसहित।"** | Return slowly along the same line |
| 42–58s | Re-anchor high on the other side and do the opposite diagonal (lift-and-turn one way, tuck-and-turn the other) | **"ब्यान्डलाई अर्को छेउ माथि सारेर उल्टो छड्के चाल गर्नुहोस्।"** | Re-anchor · The opposite diagonal |
| 58–65s | Rest | **"सकिएपछि आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest when done |
| 65–70s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy or arm tingling |

**Don't include:** big fast sweeps — the diagonals are slow and smooth, small–moderate range.
**Modification:** the physio will show the exact anchor points and patterns; practise each single direction (#2771–2775) first.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 5. Cervical Rotation with Resistance (Prone) · app exercise #2787

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist face down, head turned to one side, lifting it slightly and holding against gravity | **"यो घोप्टो परेर टाउको एक छेउ घुमाएर थोरै उठाउने अभ्यास हो।"** | Cervical Rotation with Resistance (prone) |
| 5–14s | Lying face down, forehead area supported, head turned to look toward one shoulder, cheek near the towel | **"घोप्टो पल्टनुहोस्। टाउकोलाई एउटा काँधतिर घुमाउनुहोस्, गाला तौलियाको नजिक।"** | Face down · Head turned toward one shoulder |
| 14–26s | Lift the head a small way off the surface, keeping it turned — the neck rotators work against gravity | **"टाउकोलाई घुमाएकै अवस्थामा बिस्तारै थोरै माथि उठाउनुहोस्।"** | Lift a small way · Keep it turned |
| 26–34s | Brief hold, then lower with control | **"छोटो समय समात्नुहोस्, अनि बिस्तारै तल राख्नुहोस्।"** | Brief hold · Lower with control |
| 34–46s | Turn the head the other way and repeat | **"टाउकोलाई अर्को तर्फ घुमाएर यही चाल गर्नुहोस्।"** | Turn the other way · Repeat |
| 46–55s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy or arm tingling |

**Don't include:** a big lift or forcing more rotation while lifted — small, controlled.
**Modification:** turn less far; lift barely off the surface.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 6. Multi-planar Cervical Perturbation Training · app exercise #2793

**Target: 45–55 seconds.** Needs a partner.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist seated, neck neutral; a partner gives quick light taps to the head from different directions; the head barely moves | **"यो अभ्यासमा सहयोगीले टाउकोमा अचानक हल्का धक्का दिन्छन्, तपाईंले घाँटी स्थिर राख्नुपर्छ।"** | Perturbation Training (needs a partner) |
| 6–16s | Sitting tall, gentle chin tuck, eyes forward, whole spine relaxed but ready | **"बलियो कुर्सीमा सिधा बस्नुहोस्, चिउँडो हल्का भित्रतिर, हेराइ अगाडि।"** | Sit tall · Gentle chin tuck · Ready |
| 16–30s | Partner taps lightly and unpredictably — forehead, back of head, each side — you resist and return to neutral each time | **"सहयोगीले निधार, पछाडि, दुवै छेउमा हल्का र अनपेक्षित रूपमा छुन्छन् — हरेक पटक घाँटीलाई सामान्य स्थितिमा फर्काउनुहोस्।"** | Small, unpredictable taps · Return to neutral each time |
| 30–40s | Taps get a little quicker as control improves — still light | **"नियन्त्रण सुध्रिँदै जाँदा धक्का अलि छिटो हुन्छ — तर हल्कै।"** | A little quicker as control improves — still light |
| 40–48s | Stop, rest, eyes forward | **"रोक्नुहोस्, सिधा हेरेर आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Stop · Rest · Eyes forward |
| 48–55s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा, टाउको दुख्ने वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा तुरुन्तै रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop at once if dizzy, headache, or arm tingling |

**Don't include:** hard shoves or jerky forces from the partner — these are light, controlled nudges, never enough to snap the head.
**Modification:** partner uses one finger and a very small force; start with predictable taps before unpredictable.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा, टाउको दुख्ने वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 7. Dynamic Visual Fixation · app exercise #2794

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist seated, eyes locked on a target held at arm's length, moving the head side to side while the eyes stay on the target | **"यो अभ्यासमा आँखालाई एउटा बिन्दुमा अडाएर टाउको चलाइन्छ।"** | Dynamic Visual Fixation |
| 6–16s | Sitting tall, hold a small target (a letter on a card) at arm's length, eyes on it | **"बलियो कुर्सीमा सिधा बस्नुहोस्। एउटा सानो निशाना हातको दूरीमा समातेर आँखा त्यसैमा राख्नुहोस्।"** | Hold a small target at arm's length · Eyes on it |
| 16–28s | Slowly turn the head left and right — the eyes stay fixed on the target the whole time (the target stays clear) | **"टाउकोलाई बिस्तारै दायाँ-बायाँ घुमाउनुहोस् — आँखा निशानामै अडिरहोस्, निशाना धमिलो नहोस्।"** | Turn the head · Eyes stay locked on the target |
| 28–38s | Then slowly nod the head up and down, eyes still fixed | **"अनि टाउकोलाई बिस्तारै माथि-तल गर्नुहोस्, आँखा उही ठाउँमा।"** | Then up and down · Eyes still fixed |
| 38–48s | Rest, eyes forward | **"सकिएपछि सिधा हेरेर आराम गर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest · Eyes forward |
| 48–55s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा वा वाकवाकी लाग्यो भने रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy or nauseous |

**Don't include:** fast head turns that blur the target — slow enough that the target always stays sharp.
**Modification:** smaller head range; start with the target on a wall rather than held in the hand.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा वाकवाकी लाग्यो भने रोक्नुहोस्।

## 8. Gaze Stability Training (VOR) · app exercise #2795

**Target: 55–65 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist seated, a target on the wall, moving the head faster while keeping the target in sharp focus | **"यो Gaze Stability अभ्यास हो। टाउको छिटो चलाउँदा पनि निशाना सफा देखिनुपर्छ।"** | Gaze Stability (VOR) |
| 6–16s | Sitting tall, a small clear target on the wall at eye level, arm's length away, eyes on it | **"बलियो कुर्सीमा सिधा बस्नुहोस्। भित्तामा आँखाको उचाइमा सानो सफा निशाना, हातको दूरीमा। आँखा त्यसैमा।"** | Small target on the wall · Eyes on it |
| 16–30s | Turn the head left–right at a comfortable speed — the target must stay sharp; if it blurs, slow down | **"टाउकोलाई आरामदायी गतिमा दायाँ-बायाँ घुमाउनुहोस् — निशाना सफा रहनुपर्छ। धमिलो भए ढिलो गर्नुहोस्।"** | Turn left–right · Keep the target sharp |
| 30–42s | Gradually increase the speed only as far as the target stays clear; then repeat up–down | **"निशाना सफा रहेसम्म मात्र गति बढाउनुहोस्। अनि माथि-तल पनि गर्नुहोस्।"** | Faster only while the target stays clear · Then up–down |
| 42–54s | Rest, look around the room normally | **"सकिएपछि कोठा वरिपरि सामान्य रूपमा हेर्नुहोस्।"** | Rest · Look around normally |
| 54–65s | Safety screen | **"रिंगटा, वाकवाकी वा टाउको दुख्ने भयो भने रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop if dizzy, nauseous, or headache |

**Don't include:** pushing the speed so the target smears — the whole point is a stable, clear image.
**Modification:** start seated and slow; the physio may prescribe a specific speed and duration.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा, वाकवाकी वा टाउको दुख्ने भयो भने रोक्नुहोस्।

## 9. Cervical Endurance Test (Hold positions) · app exercise #2796

**Target: 55–65 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist holding a supine chin-tuck head-lift, timing it, then a prone extension hold | **"यो घाँटीका मांसपेशी कति लामो समय राम्ररी काम गर्न सक्छन् भनेर जाँच्ने अभ्यास हो।"** | Cervical Endurance — timed holds |
| 6–20s | Supine chin tuck with a small head lift — hold and time how long the tuck stays perfect (no chin poke, no shake) | **"उत्तानो पल्टेर Chin Tuck सँगै सानो टाउको उठाउनुहोस् — जबसम्म तान राम्रो रहन्छ, त्यति समय गन्नुहोस्।"** | Hold 1: supine chin tuck lift — time it |
| 20–34s | Rest, then a prone cervical extension hold — time how long the small lift and long neck hold | **"आराम गरेर, अनि घोप्टो Extension होल्ड — कति समय राम्ररी समात्न सकिन्छ गन्नुहोस्।"** | Hold 2: prone extension — time it |
| 34–48s | Rest, then a side-lying lateral flexion hold, each side | **"अनि Side-Lying Lateral Flexion होल्ड, दुवै छेउ।"** | Hold 3: side-lying lateral flexion — each side |
| 48–58s | Note the times — the physio uses them to track progress | **"समयहरू टिप्नुहोस् — physiotherapist ले प्रगति नाप्न प्रयोग गर्नुहुन्छ।"** | Note the times for your physio |
| 58–65s | Safety screen | **"तान बिग्रिएपछि, वा रिंगटा भए तुरुन्तै रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop the moment form breaks, or if dizzy |

**Don't include:** grinding out extra seconds with bad form — the time only counts while the position is held cleanly.
**Modification:** the physio picks which positions to test and how; not all need doing every session.
**Safety line:** रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भएमा रोक्नुहोस्।

## 10. Supine Chin Tuck with Respiratory Challenge · app exercise #2797

**Target: 45–55 seconds.**

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Therapist supine, holding a chin tuck while taking slow deep belly breaths | **"यो Chin Tuck समात्दै गहिरो पेटको सास फेर्ने अभ्यास हो।"** | Chin Tuck + breathing challenge |
| 5–14s | Hook-lying, one hand on the belly, hold a gentle chin tuck | **"उत्तानो पल्टनुहोस्, एउटा हात पेटमा। हल्का Chin Tuck समात्नुहोस्।"** | Hold a gentle chin tuck · Hand on the belly |

**Target: 45–55 seconds.** Needs a Swiss ball and a partner.

| Time | Visual | Voice-over | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6s | Therapist sitting on a Swiss ball, neck neutral; a partner gently bounces / nudges the ball; the head stays steady | **"यो Swiss ball मा बसेर, सहयोगीले बल हल्लाउँदा घाँटी स्थिर राख्ने अभ्यास हो।"** | Perturbation on a Swiss ball (needs a partner + ball) |

| 48–55s | Safety screen | **"सन्तुलन बिग्रिने, रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भए तुरुन्तै रोक्नुहोस्।"** | Stop at once if you lose balance, feel dizzy, or arm tingling |

**Safety line:** सन्तुलन बिग्रिने, रिंगटा वा हातमा झमझमाहट भए रोक्नुहोस्।

# Section B — Reference only (not solo home exercises)

These use gym/specialist equipment or load the neck heavily. Any video is
a form reference filmed with a coach present, carrying:

> **तपाईंको physiotherapist वा प्रशिक्षकको उपस्थितिमा मात्र — भिडियो हेरेर एक्लै नगर्नुहोस्।**

### 13. 4-Way Neck Machine (Isotonic) · #2788
**What:** A gym neck machine loading flexion, extension, side flexion and (some) rotation through a padded lever.
**Cue:** Seat and pad set so the axis is at the neck, not the jaw · light plate to start · move through a controlled mid-range, no end-range slamming · matched sets in opposite directions.
**Hard stop:** any pinch, dizziness, or the head being forced past a comfortable range by the weight.

### 14. Neck Harness Lifts (Weight plate) · #2789
**What:** A head harness with a plate hanging from a chain — nodding / extending / side-bending against the hanging load. **Strength & conditioning, not rehab.**
**Cue:** Very light plate · seated, torso fixed · small controlled range · never a bouncing or jerking rep.
**Hard stop:** loss of control of the plate, or any neck pain.

### 15. Iron Neck Training (Rotational) · #2790
**What:** A helmet device with a resistance dial, for anti-rotation and resisted turning drills. **Athletic conditioning.**
**Cue:** Low resistance · trunk still, the neck does the work · smooth resisted turns and holds · no fast whipping.
**Hard stop:** dizziness, headache, or pain — remove the device.

### 16. Wrestler's Bridge (Full) · #2791 — NOT filmed as an instructional video
Full bodyweight loaded through the cervical spine in extension. High risk of serious neck injury on a failed rep. This is combat-athlete conditioning, built up over years with coaching — it is **not** an exercise to teach a patient (or a general user) from a video, and arguably doesn't belong in a rehab exercise library at all. If it stays in the catalog, gate it to coached athletes only and do not produce a "how-to" clip.

### 17. Headstand (Tripod) · #2792 — NOT filmed as an instructional video
Same reasoning as #2791: axial load through the head and cervical spine, high consequence if it fails. Not appropriate as patient-facing video content.
